T-money guide

How To Use T-money In Korea

T-money is one of the simplest ways to pay for subway and bus travel in Korea. The practical questions are where to buy it, how to add balance, and whether a physical card or Apple Wallet makes more sense for your trip.

How it works

Buy, Recharge And Tap

A physical card can be ready soon after arrival. Buying the card and adding balance are separate steps.

1Buy

Physical cards are commonly available at participating convenience stores and subway customer or service centers.

2Recharge

Add balance at a subway recharge machine or participating convenience store. Cash is the most predictable method for a physical card.

3Tap

Tap when entering and exiting subway gates. Tap again when leaving the bus.

4Balance

The remaining balance appears at many gates and recharge machines.

What is T-money?

Subway, Bus, Taxi And Some Stores

Public transport is the card's main job. Acceptance at taxis and retail partners can vary.

Subway

Tap at the entry gate and again at the exit gate. The second tap closes the journey and calculates the fare.

Bus

Tap when boarding and again when getting off. The exit tap matters when a transfer discount applies.

Taxi

Many taxis accept T-money, but another payment method is useful in case a particular vehicle cannot process it.

Stores

Selected convenience stores and other partner merchants accept T-money. It is not a general-purpose shopping card, and individual locations may differ.

Where to buy

Where To Buy A Physical T-money Card

Most visitors can buy one after arrival without arranging a pickup in advance.

Physical T-money cards are commonly available at participating convenience stores and subway customer or service centers. Airport convenience stores can be a practical first stop after landing, although stock and card designs vary by outlet.

Buying a card is different from recharging it. Subway recharge machines are common places to add balance, but a recharge machine should not be treated as a guaranteed card vending machine.

Some travel platforms sell cards or bundles for airport pickup. That arrangement is mainly useful when a particular pickup point or bundled product already suits the trip. After a late flight, the collection desk may close before immigration and baggage claim are finished.

Recharge

How To Recharge T-money

Physical cards and T-money in Apple Wallet use different top-up paths.

Physical T-money

Subway recharge machines and participating convenience stores are common top-up points. Cash remains the most predictable method for overseas visitors, while the exact payment options can differ by machine or outlet.

A physical card can be easier when phone compatibility is not worth checking, the group needs one card per person, or everyone is carrying overseas-issued payment cards.

T-money In Apple Wallet

T-money has been available in Apple Wallet since July 21, 2025. Once it is set up and funded, a supported iPhone or Apple Watch can be tapped as the transit card.

Adding money directly in Apple Wallet currently requires a Korean-issued credit or debit card that works with Apple Pay. An overseas-issued Visa or Mastercard is not a dependable Wallet top-up method. This limits how easily many visitors can add balance; it does not mean that Apple Wallet T-money is unavailable.

Five T-money Problems That Are Easy To Avoid

Most problems come from a missed tap, a low balance or confusing a recharge point with another payment service.

Subway gates need two taps

The entry tap begins the journey and the exit tap closes it. Missing the second tap can leave the fare record incomplete.

Bus transfers depend on the exit tap

Tapping again when leaving the bus records the end of that ride and matters when a transfer discount applies.

Physical-card recharge may still need cash

An overseas credit card is not a dependable default at every recharge point. A small amount of Korean cash avoids having to search for another machine or outlet.

A low balance can interrupt the next journey

The remaining amount appears at many gates and recharge machines. Looking at it before a longer ride leaves time to add balance without blocking the gate.

Single-use tickets are not reusable transport cards

A single-use subway ticket covers the current journey, while T-money keeps a rechargeable balance for later trips. The machine label matters before payment.

Refund

Refunding The Remaining Balance

Refund locations, eligible amounts, fees and treatment of the physical card price depend on current T-money rules.

Before leaving Korea, a participating convenience store, subway customer or service center, or official T-money service point can explain which refund channel handles the remaining balance. Limits and fees differ by location, and the purchase price of the physical card may be treated separately. The current rule should be confirmed rather than assuming every outlet can process the same refund.

T-money FAQ

Questions Tourists Ask

These questions focus on buying, recharging and using T-money as a foreign visitor.

What is T-money?

T-money is a rechargeable transit card used on subways and buses across much of Korea. It is also accepted by many taxis and selected partner merchants, although acceptance can vary outside the core transport network.

Where can I buy a T-money card?

Physical T-money cards are commonly available at participating convenience stores and subway customer or service centers. Airport convenience stores can also be useful after arrival. Subway recharge machines are mainly for adding balance, so do not assume every machine also sells the card.

Can I use T-money on my iPhone or Apple Watch?

Yes. Since July 21, 2025, T-money has been available in Apple Wallet on supported iPhone and Apple Watch devices. Once set up and funded, the device can be tapped as the transit card.

Can I recharge Apple Wallet T-money with a foreign credit card?

Adding money directly to T-money in Apple Wallet currently requires a Korean-issued credit or debit card that works with Apple Pay. An overseas-issued Visa or Mastercard is therefore not a dependable Wallet top-up method, even though the T-money card itself can be used after it has balance.

How do I recharge a physical T-money card?

Physical T-money cards can be recharged at subway recharge machines and participating convenience stores. Cash remains the most predictable method for overseas visitors, while the exact payment options can differ by machine or outlet.

Can I use T-money on buses, subways and taxis?

Yes. Tap at entry and exit on the subway, and tap when boarding and leaving a bus. Many taxis accept T-money, but another payment method is useful in case a particular taxi cannot process it.

Should I choose T-money, WOWPASS or a Climate Card?

T-money leaves routes flexible and works well for pay-as-you-go transport beyond Seoul. WOWPASS combines a visitor payment card with a separate T-money balance. A short-term Climate Card covers eligible Seoul public transport for 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days, so it is most relevant when the itinerary stays within its service area and uses transit heavily.

Can I get a refund on my remaining T-money balance?

Refund channels, amount limits and fees vary. A participating convenience store, subway customer or service center, or official T-money service point can explain which channel handles the balance, while the physical card price may be treated separately under current rules.

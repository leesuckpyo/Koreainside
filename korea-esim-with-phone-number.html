Korean carrier comparison

Korea eSIM with a Korean Phone Number

A Korean tourist eSIM is worth considering when someone in Korea actually needs to call or text you, or when you need a domestic number for a reservation or local contact. If all you need is maps, messages, translation and internet access, a regular data eSIM is usually simpler.

The important question is not only whether the plan gives you a Korean number. You also need to know whether it can receive SMS, whether you can make outgoing calls, whether passport or airport verification is required, and whether the service you want will accept a tourist number at all.

This guide compares official tourist eSIM conditions from SK Telecom, KT and LG U+. A carrier passport or entry check activates carrier services; it is not the same as Korean resident identity verification.

Quick answer

Start with what you need the number to do

A Korean number can solve one travel problem without solving every Korean verification problem.

If data is all you need

If the phone is mainly for maps, messaging, translation, browsing and app-based calls, a Korean number may add complexity without changing much about the trip. In that case, a normal travel data eSIM is usually the easier choice.

If someone in Korea needs to call or text you

A tourist carrier eSIM becomes more useful when a hotel, driver, restaurant or local service needs a domestic contact number, or when receiving a Korean SMS genuinely matters. At that point, check incoming and outgoing functions separately because they are not always bundled together.

If you are buying it for verification

Be careful here. Receiving a text message and completing Korean resident identity verification are different things. A tourist number may work for some simple SMS codes while still being unusable for banking, government or resident-only authentication.

Phone functions

Do you need to receive calls and texts, or send them too?

Some travelers only need a Korean number so a hotel, restaurant or driver can reach them. Others need to make domestic calls or send SMS themselves. Those are different requirements, and they can lead to different products even within the same carrier.

Incoming service may be available on a product that does not offer outgoing calls or texts. Outgoing use can require a voice-capable plan, a separate balance or an additional passport or entry check.

Before comparing SK Telecom, KT and LG U+, decide whether receiving is enough or whether you genuinely need to call and text from the Korean number. That single distinction removes a lot of unnecessary complexity.

Know the boundary

What a Korean 010 number actually gives you

A number can make local contact easier

A Korean mobile number can make it easier for a hotel, driver or local business to reach you, and some tourist products can receive calls or SMS after the carrier's required verification is complete. The exact functions still depend on the carrier and product.

Outgoing use is a separate question

Making calls, sending texts, adding a voice balance and completing the required activation steps are not identical across the three carriers. A plan that gives you a number does not automatically mean every call and SMS function is ready.

A 010 number is not a Korean identity

A working Korean number does not create Korean resident identity records. Banking, government services and other identity-based systems can require subscriber and resident information that a short-term tourist line does not provide.

Product type

Data-only or data-and-voice?

  1. 01
    Data-only

    A carrier's data-only tourist eSIM can still be useful when you want local-network data and possibly some incoming functions, but outgoing voice and SMS may be limited or unavailable. Do not assume “Korean carrier eSIM” automatically means full phone service.

  2. 02
    Data + Voice

    A voice-capable product is the better fit when you expect to make domestic calls or send SMS yourself. These products can involve additional verification, airport procedures or a separate calling balance, so the extra capability comes with more setup.

Setup choice

Do you want to finish setup online, or are you okay stopping at the airport?

The three carriers do not handle voice and SMS activation in exactly the same way. That matters more than it may seem after a long flight.

SK Telecom allows online purchase and provides a path for passport verification after immigration, while airport support is also available. KT allows online data purchase but routes the entry check for voice and SMS through its airport roaming center. LG U+ separates its online Data Only option from the airport-supported Data + Voice product.

If avoiding an airport counter is important, compare the complete activation path before comparing the carrier name. If you would rather have staff confirm the line, calls and texts before you leave the terminal, an airport-supported setup may actually be the easier choice.

Activation timing

When does the Korean number actually become usable?

Buying the eSIM, installing the QR code, seeing the Korean number, starting mobile data and enabling calls or SMS can happen at different times.

A traveler may have the eSIM profile installed before the flight but still need to arrive in Korea, connect to the network or complete the carrier's passport or entry check before every function becomes available.

That is why the useful question is not only “Does this plan include a Korean number?” Check when the number appears, when data validity starts and what must happen before incoming or outgoing calls and texts work.

Critical distinction

SMS codes are not the same as Korean identity verification

  1. 01
    A Korean number exists

    The carrier has assigned a mobile number to the line. That only means the number exists; it does not tell you what another app or service will do with it.

  2. 02
    The line can receive SMS

    The product and carrier settings allow a text to arrive after any required verification. Some products may have more limited messaging functions.

  3. 03
    The app accepts that number

    A restaurant, taxi or reservation app may accept a tourist number and send a simple code. Another service may reject the same number under a different policy.

  4. 04
    The service verifies your legal identity

    This is the step tourist eSIMs generally cannot promise. Banking, government and resident services can require Korean subscriber-name and identity records that are separate from simply owning a 010 number.

Official carrier guidance distinguishes simple call or SMS service from personal authentication. Treat each app as a separate case rather than assuming that one successful text means every Korean verification service will work.

Multiple lines

Buying several lines for a family? Check the passport rule first

Buying several tourist eSIMs is not always the same as verifying several lines under one passport. Carrier purchase limits and passport-verification limits can be different.

SK Telecom and KT place restrictions around how tourist lines are verified with passport or entry information, while LG U+ publishes its own purchase limits. If you are buying for a couple, family or multiple devices, check the exact carrier rule before paying for several lines under one person's passport.

Do not assume that one passport can activate unlimited voice-and-SMS lines simply because multiple products can be purchased.

Carrier details

What differs by carrier

SK Telecom tourist eSIM

SK Telecom is worth comparing if you want to buy online and would prefer to complete the remaining passport step after you arrive rather than making the airport counter the only path. Its tourist lineup separates data service from a product that adds calls and SMS.

The important timing difference is that data and phone functions are not necessarily ready at the same moment. The checked official guidance allows data use before passport verification, while calls and texts depend on the post-entry verification step and outgoing use requires the appropriate balance.

The trade-off is that this is still a tourist service, not a resident identity line. Personal authentication and some payment-related messages are restricted, and deleting the eSIM can require buying a new one.

KT tourist eSIM

KT stands out when having a clearly identified 010 tourist number matters and you are comfortable using airport support for the voice and SMS side of the service. Data can be purchased online, but the carrier's checked English guidance sends the entry check for phone functions to a KT airport roaming center.

Once that step is complete, the voice-capable product can handle incoming service and outgoing use through the required balance. KT also publishes extension and top-up paths, which can matter on a longer stay.

The main trade-off is the airport step and the same verification boundary that applies across tourist lines: having the number does not provide Korean personal authentication. A deleted profile also has strict reissue limits.

Official KT tourist eSIM page · KT roaming customer center

LG U+ tourist eSIM

LG U+ is useful when you want a clear choice between an online Data Only setup and a more complete Data + Voice option supported at the airport. Its official tourist guidance lists incoming calls and texts on the relevant products, while outgoing use belongs to the voice-capable option.

The assigned number becomes visible after arrival, and the usage period is tied to first data use under the current guidance. Hotspot support, eligible extension and voice top-up can make the service flexible once it is active.

The biggest limitation is what the number cannot become. LG U+ distinguishes simple reservation SMS use from banking or government identity verification, and an installed or activated eSIM has strict deletion and reissue limits.

Setup path

Online activation versus airport support

Purchase, QR installation, data start and voice/SMS activation are not always the same step.

  1. SKT

    Buy online, then verify after immigration

    The email voucher carries the QR code and passport-verification path. Travelers can complete that check online during SK Telecom's stated hours or use an airport roaming center.

  2. KT

    Use the airport roaming center for phone functions

    Data eSIM is available online. Voice and SMS require KT's airport entry check, so the passport and purchase information need to be ready at the counter.

  3. LG U+

    Choose online Data Only or airport Data + Voice

    Advance Data Only purchases provide a QR code, with validity starting at first data use. LG U+ lists the voice-capable option for airport purchase.

A practical exception

Sometimes the airport counter is the simpler option

An eSIM is often convenient because there is no physical card to swap, but voice and SMS can make the setup less “instant” than the word eSIM suggests.

If you only need data, setting up online before departure is usually attractive. If you absolutely need working calls or texts as soon as you arrive, an airport carrier counter or even a staffed physical-SIM setup can be less stressful because someone can confirm the line before you leave.

The better choice is the setup you can rely on, not the format that sounds more modern.

Final checks

Before you buy

  • Make sure the phone supports eSIM and is unlocked before paying.
  • Know whether receiving is enough or you need to call and text out.
  • Allow time for any passport or airport entry check tied to phone service.
  • Find out whether installation, network connection or first data use starts validity.
  • Read the refund, deletion and reissue terms before scanning the QR code.
  • Keep the QR code, voucher and purchase record somewhere available offline.
  • If you plan to tether another device, verify hotspot support for that product.
  • Set the Korean eSIM as the data line so the home SIM does not use paid roaming data.
  • Treat a 010 number as a communication tool, not proof that identity verification will work.

Prevent avoidable problems

Mistakes that cause the most trouble

Buying data-only when outgoing calls are required

The number on a data-oriented product does not automatically include outgoing service. Look for the voice-capable product and find out whether it needs a separate balance.

Assuming incoming SMS means full app verification

A text can reach the phone while the app still rejects the tourist number. Number assignment, SMS delivery, app acceptance and legal identity are separate hurdles.

Confusing entry check with resident identity verification

The carrier's check turns on its calls and texts. It does not create the Korean subscriber and resident records used by banking or government systems.

Deleting an installed eSIM

All three carriers publish deletion or reissue limits. Turning the line off is safer than deleting it while you troubleshoot, and carrier support should be the next step.

Activating before the intended date

Scanning a QR code, installing the profile and starting the plan are not always the same event. Read the timing rule before doing any of them early.

Arriving without passport or purchase information

An airport or post-entry check is much harder when the email cannot be loaded. Keep the passport, voucher, contract number and QR record available offline.

Assuming every plan can be extended

Extension rules differ by carrier and product. SK Telecom's checked eSIM guidance sends travelers to a new purchase, while KT and LG U+ publish eligible extension paths.

Expecting the same call balance from every carrier

Outgoing calls and texts can draw from different balances or product types. Check the selected carrier's recharge method before relying on the line.

Leaving the home SIM as the data line

A correctly installed Korean eSIM will not prevent the other SIM from roaming. Set the Korean line for mobile data and turn off automatic cellular-data switching.

Choosing a Korean carrier plan when data-only is simpler

Maps, messaging and app-based calls do not need a Korean number. Adding carrier verification and voice rules for those uses can create work without adding practical value.

Simpler alternative

When an international data eSIM is the better choice

A Korean carrier plan is unnecessary if the phone only needs maps, messaging, translation and ordinary mobile data. In that situation, an international travel eSIM can avoid the extra questions about phone numbers, voice balance and carrier verification.

It can also be simpler for travelers continuing to Japan or another country, or for anyone who wants to finish setup before the flight and avoid an airport counter.

Bottom line

The simplest way to decide

If you only need internet access, start with a regular travel eSIM. A Korean tourist eSIM becomes worth the extra setup when receiving local calls or texts, making domestic calls, or having a Korean contact number will actually change the trip.

If you do need the Korean number, compare incoming and outgoing functions first, then the passport or airport process, and only then the carrier. A tourist 010 number can make local communication easier; it does not turn the line into Korean resident identity.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do Korean tourist eSIMs provide a 010 phone number?

Not every carrier documents the number in the same way. KT explicitly says all of its tourist eSIM numbers use the 010 prefix. The checked SK Telecom English page confirms a mobile number and phone functions but does not clearly state the 010 prefix, while LG U+ says its assigned number appears after arrival in Korea. Check the exact product before paying.

Can I receive calls and SMS with a Korean tourist eSIM?

Often, but the setup differs. SK Telecom enables incoming calls and texts after passport information verification. KT requires an airport entry check before voice and SMS work. LG U+ lists incoming calls and texts for both Data Only and Data + Voice products.

Can I make outgoing calls and send SMS?

It depends on the carrier and product. SK Telecom's Data, Call, SMS product and KT's voice-capable eSIM require the appropriate calling or text balance. LG U+ reserves outgoing use for its Data + Voice product. A data-only product may not let you call or text out.

Can I use a tourist eSIM for Korean app verification?

Some simple SMS codes may work if the line can receive texts and the app accepts a tourist number. LG U+ specifically mentions restaurant and taxi reservation SMS. Each service sets its own policy, so one successful code does not mean every Korean app will accept the same number.

Can I use it for banking or government identity verification?

No. Do not rely on a tourist eSIM for banking, government or resident identity verification. SK Telecom restricts personal authentication and payment-related texts, KT says personal authentication is unavailable, and LG U+ excludes banking and government identity verification.

Do I need a passport to activate the eSIM?

The requirement depends on the carrier and function. SK Telecom requires passport information verification after immigration before calls and texts work. KT sends travelers to an airport roaming center for its voice-and-SMS entry check. LG U+ uses passport-based purchase or registration conditions and sells Data + Voice at the airport.

Can I install the eSIM before arriving in Korea?

Some online products provide a QR code before arrival, but installation, validity and phone activation are separate events. SK Telecom sends its online QR voucher by email and completes passport verification after immigration. LG U+ allows advance Data Only purchase and starts the usage period at first data use. Follow the selected carrier's timing instructions before scanning.

What happens if I delete the eSIM?

Deletion can permanently disable the profile. SK Telecom requires a new eSIM purchase, KT does not provide a new QR code after deletion, and LG U+ does not reinstall or reissue an installed or activated eSIM. Disable the line instead, and contact the carrier before deleting anything.

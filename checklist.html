What do I actually need before flying to Korea?

Safety and emergency

Save emergency steps before you need them

Emergencies are harder when your phone, wallet, passport or data is missing.

Service Number When to use it
Police 112 Crime, immediate danger, serious loss report or police assistance.
Fire and ambulance 119 Fire, rescue, ambulance or urgent medical emergency.
Korea Travel Helpline 1330 Tourist information, interpretation help and travel complaints.
Medical information 1339 Check current availability before relying on it; use 119 for urgent emergencies.

Keep this offline

Save 1330 and your accommodation phone number before travel. Visit Korea lists +82-2-1330 for calls from overseas.

If you get sick

  • Minor symptoms: Visit a pharmacy and use a translation app to describe symptoms clearly.
  • Serious symptoms: Use 119 or go to an emergency room. Ask your accommodation to help with the address and interpretation if needed.
  • Insurance: Contact your travel insurance provider before major treatment when possible, and keep receipts.

If you lose something

  • On transit: Note the line, station, bus route, taxi receipt or time window before asking for help.
  • Wallet or phone: Freeze cards quickly and ask staff to help contact the operator.
  • Passport: Contact local police and your embassy or consulate as soon as possible.

FAQ

Korea travel checklist FAQ

Short answers to the decisions travelers most often need to make before departure.

What should I prepare before flying to Korea?

Confirm entry requirements, mobile data, airport transport, payment backups and your accommodation route. Save booking, insurance and emergency information offline before departure.

Should I install an eSIM before arriving in Korea?

Install one before departure if your phone supports eSIM and you want data as soon as you land. Otherwise, decide where you will buy a SIM before leaving the airport.

Which apps should I download before visiting Korea?

Start with a Korea map app and a translation app. Add taxi or payment apps only when they match your transport and payment plans.

How should I choose transport from Incheon Airport?

Choose by destination, arrival time and luggage. Compare AREX, airport bus and taxi on the airport transfer guide instead of choosing only by price.

Do I need a T-money card in Korea?

T-money is useful if you expect to use subway or bus regularly. You may need it less if you mainly walk, use taxis or rely on private transport.

Can I rely only on a foreign credit card in Korea?

Foreign cards work in many places, but not everywhere. Carry a second card and some backup cash, and keep transport payment separate from everyday spending.

What should I check before booking accommodation in Korea?

Check the exact route from the station exit or airport bus stop to the entrance. Confirm luggage storage, check-in rules, elevator access and the address in Korean.

What emergency numbers should I save in Korea?

Save 112 for police, 119 for fire and ambulance, and 1330 for travel help. Verify public-service details with official sources before your trip.

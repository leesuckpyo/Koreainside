# 1. PAGE 31 — `where-to-stay-in-jamsil.html`

## A. Page Role

This URL should own:

> **If Jamsil is actually part of the trip, which side should the traveler stay on — Lotte World/Jamsil Station, Seokchon Lake, Bangi/Jamsil east, or the Sports Complex side — and which hotel fits the main reason for being there?**

The Stay page should not assume every Jamsil traveler is there for the same reason.

Distinct reasons include:
- Lotte World
- Seoul Sky / Lotte World Tower
- Seokchon Lake
- children/family
- baseball
- concert
- southeast-Seoul itinerary
- higher-end hotel stay

## H. Official Current Fact Audit

### Korean law
Current Road Traffic Act Article 96 allows qualifying IDP / mutually recognized foreign-license holders to drive for up to one year from entry, within permitted vehicle class.

### Taiwan-specific recognition
Taiwan Ministry of Foreign Affairs confirms:
- Taiwan and Korea signed the `臺韓國際駕照相互承認瞭解備忘錄` in February 2022;
- valid Taiwan-issued international driving permits can be used by short-term Taiwan travelers in Korea for the recognized private passenger-car scope.

LOTTE Rent-a-Car current official requirements explicitly state:
- Taiwan IDPs are valid in South Korea;
- original IDP required;
- passport required;
- current basic passenger-car age range starts at 21;
- current required payment method is debit or credit card; cash not accepted;
- vehicle class / document details vary by vehicle.

# 1. PAGE 16 — `hongdae-vs-myeongdong.html`

## A. Page Role

Own one concrete comparison:

> **For the actual trip, should the traveler stay in Hongdae or Myeongdong?**

The page should answer through:
- first-trip sightseeing
- airport arrival/departure
- late evenings
- shopping-bag return trips
- luggage
- children/family
- noise
- actual hotel position

This is a Comparison page, not a generic Seoul accommodation guide.

## B. Search Intent

Taiwan query language observed:

- `弘大還是明洞`
- `弘大 明洞 哪個好`
- `弘大 明洞 住宿`
- `弘大 明洞 比較`
- `第一次去首爾住弘大還是明洞`

- where to sleep
- where they will repeatedly end the day
- what matters more: airport/west-side evenings or central-city flexibility

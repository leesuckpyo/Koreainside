# 6. Final Cross-Page Findings — Taiwan Market

## 6.1 The Taiwan project is not primarily a “rewrite all pages” project

Across the 58 pages, the most common result was:

- strong body;
- correct decision architecture;
- weaker Taiwan search-facing terminology;
- stale/current factual module;
- missing contextual internal link;
- localized search-language mismatch.

This supports a **surgical restructuring** approach.

## 6.2 Korea Inside’s strongest moat is still decision support

Repeated advantages:
- who should choose this;
- who should not;
- luggage;
- exit;
- transfer;
- final walk;
- room/bed/occupancy;
- queue;
- weather;
- timing;
- late-night return;
- booking condition;
- alternative;
- exception.

Taiwan competitors often win on:
- list volume;
- `必買 / 必吃 / 必玩`;
- hotel counts;
- live commercial inventory.

Korea Inside should not copy those strengths mechanically.

## 6.3 Taiwan search language should be used at the surface

- `住宿推薦`
- `逛街`
- `一日遊`
- `美食推薦`
- `親子住宿`
- `獨旅住宿`
- `情侶住宿`
- `平價住宿`
- `奢華住宿`
- `eSIM 推薦`
- `010號碼`
- `韓國旅遊 App 推薦`
- `韓國付款方式`

These terms can improve search entry while deeper body content retains Korea Inside’s editorial style.

## 6.4 Taiwan localization must not contain English-market default examples

Confirmed examples found during this research:
- `美國發行卡`
- `我的美國 Apple Pay 卡片`
- `美國旅客`

These are understandable Traditional Chinese, but not correct Taiwan-default framing.

# 56. `taste-korea.html`

## Current English role

Current title:
**Taste Korea: Food Experiences, Cities & Where to Stay**

Current H1:
**Taste Korea**

The page is not a `Top 20 Korean Foods` list.  
Its role is a **food-travel decision hub**:

- market day
- Korean barbecue
- café day
- regional dishes
- seafood / coast
- cooking class / food tour
- Seoul neighborhood choice
- route and stay implications

This role is strategically useful and should be preserved.

## Indonesian search language

Broad high-interest wording:
- `makanan Korea`
- `kuliner Korea`
- `makanan Korea Selatan`
- `makanan Korea yang wajib dicoba`
- `wisata kuliner Korea`
- `kuliner Seoul`
- `makanan Korea untuk wisatawan`

The broad `makanan Korea` intent often returns dish-list content.  
Korea Inside should not destroy the page's decision-hub role just to imitate those listicles.

## Current SERP competitor set

### Igeo Juseyo
Page: **Panduan Makanan Korea untuk Pemula**.  
Type: direct Indonesia-language food/travel editorial competitor.  
Strength:
- beginner orientation
- dishes to try
- ordering habits
- dietary considerations

### Seoul Window Indonesia
Page: **15 Makanan Korea yang Selalu Direkomendasikan Warga Lokal ke Orang Asing**.  
Type: strong Indonesia-language Korea editorial competitor.  
Strength:
- dish-specific depth
- locations
- current fact-checking
- strong list intent

### LACHA Indonesia
Page: **12 Makanan Korea yang Wajib Dicoba — Di Mana, Berapa Harganya, dan Cara Memesannya**.  
Type: direct food-guide competitor.  
Strength:
- dish + price + ordering + solo-eating + dietary constraints

### Howliday / tiket.com
Type: broader Indonesian travel/food publishers.
Strength:
- familiar Indonesian query wording
- `makanan`, `kuliner`, `halal`, `street food`

## 1:1 SEO GAP

Korea Inside's advantage is different:
- what kind of food day the traveler wants
- which neighborhood/city fits that day
- how food changes route and stay decisions
- market vs barbecue vs café vs regional food

The risk is query mismatch if Indonesian localization uses only a literal `Taste Korea`.

Recommended future search ownership:
> **wisata kuliner Korea / panduan kuliner Korea untuk wisatawan**

rather than trying to win `makanan Korea` solely as a dish encyclopedia.

Search-facing copy should establish the page as:
- food-experience planner
- where to eat by trip style / area
- route-making guide

## Indonesia-specific opportunity — Muslim search demand

Current Indonesian SERP strongly surfaces:
- `makanan halal Korea`
- Muslim-friendly food
- pork/alcohol/broth concerns

This reinforces the previously identified candidate:
**Muslim-Friendly Seoul / Halal Food Seoul**

Do **not** inject generic halal claims into this page.

Rules remain:
- halal ≠ pork-free
- certification / self-certified / Muslim-friendly differ
- restaurant-level proof required

This is still a separate **research candidate**, not an approved page.

## GEO / AI-answer readiness

Current page is strong for:
- “What kind of food experience should I build into my trip?”
- market vs barbecue vs café vs regional choice

For Indonesian AI answers, search-facing wording should make the role explicit because `Taste Korea` alone is semantically weak in Bahasa Indonesia.

## Internal-link role

Strong potential bridge to:
- Myeongdong
- Hongdae
- Seongsu
- Insadong
- Gongdeok / Mapo
- future regional hubs
- Stay
- payments
- tours / cooking classes when contextually matched

## 1:1 SEO GAP

Current English body has a good planning model:
**browse spontaneously vs book ahead**.

Indonesian localization should make the search intent more explicit:
- shopping (`belanja skincare/kosmetik`)
- experiences (`personal color`, salon)
- higher-caution clinic layer

Do not turn the page into:
- product ranking
- miracle skincare claims
- medical-result claims
- generic `best clinic` list

The clinic section's current cautious wording is a competitive advantage:
- total cost
- side effects
- recovery time
- aftercare
- provider
- no guaranteed result

## Current SERP competitor set

### Broad travel publishers / OTAs
Travel package and destination pages often compete for:
- `wisata Korea Selatan`
- `liburan ke Korea`
- `tempat wisata Korea`

These include package sellers, listicles and OTA/editorial hybrids.

### Webuy Travel Indonesia
Current page targets:
**Panduan Wisata Korea Selatan dari Indonesia — Seoul, Busan & Jeju 2026**
Type: tour/package commercial competitor.
Strength:
- Indonesia-origin travel framing
- broad Korea destination terms
Weakness for Korea Inside:
- package-sales orientation, not independent decision-first travel utility

### Wikivoyage Indonesia
Type: broad informational reference.
Strength:
- wide topical coverage
Weakness:
- less decision-first and less current in many execution details

### Indonesian travel editorial publishers
SERP is fragmented across destination, food, shopping, transport and package content rather than dominated by one independent practical guide.

## 1:1 SEO GAP

Korea Inside should **not** chase `tempat wisata Korea Selatan` by turning the homepage into a giant attractions list.

The homepage should own:
> **first-time practical Korea trip planning**

Best future Indonesian search-facing direction:
- `Panduan Liburan ke Korea untuk Pertama Kali`
- `Panduan Wisata Korea Selatan untuk Pemula`

The body already supports this role better than a generic `wisata Korea` list:
- where to stay
- airport → hotel
- mobile data
- T-money
- payments
- maps
- food / beauty entry points

## Architecture caution

Current Korea Inside public set is still heavily Seoul-centered.  
Do not imply equal Busan / Jeju / nationwide depth until regional hubs exist.

Navigation standard remains:
**Global Menu → Regional Hub → Detail Page**

When Busan / Jeju hubs are actually Production-ready, homepage scope can expand naturally without changing the current research conclusion.

## GEO / AI-answer readiness

Very strong for:

The first-answer cluster should stay:
1. accommodation base
2. mobile data
3. airport-to-hotel route
4. transport card

This is more useful than a generic attraction list.

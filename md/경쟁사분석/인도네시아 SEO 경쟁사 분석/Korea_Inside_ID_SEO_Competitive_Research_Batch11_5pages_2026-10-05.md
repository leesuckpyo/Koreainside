## 1:1 SEO GAP

4. Do not claim every Indonesian Visa/Mastercard will work.
5. Indonesian card-specific bank controls are issuer-dependent and should not be generalized.

## P0 — Indonesia applicability gate

This is the most important new Batch 11 finding.

Apple's current official **Apple Wallet: Apple Pay** country/region availability list does **not** include **Indonesia**.

Therefore, an Indonesian localization must not simply open with:
> “Yes, Apple Pay works in Korea.”

For the Indonesian market, the first gate is:

> **Do you already have a payment card that is legitimately supported in Apple Pay by an issuer from a supported market?**

A traveler relying only on ordinary Indonesian-issued cards should **not be assumed** to be able to add that card to Apple Pay merely because they are traveling to Korea.

This is not an error in the English page, because the English page explicitly addresses travelers who **already use Apple Pay at home**.  
It becomes an Indonesia-market applicability problem if localization removes or hides that condition.

## T-money consequence

Apple and Tmoney distinguish:
- direct T-money funding inside Apple Wallet; and
- the MobileTmoney international-user route.

Current sources show the foreign-user route as an Apple Pay-funded route with supported networks including Mastercard / Amex / UnionPay / JCB, while Visa is not currently listed.

For Indonesian users, the practical problem is upstream:
**if the traveler cannot use Apple Pay with their Indonesian-issued card, the MobileTmoney Apple Pay route should not be presented as a universal solution.**

A physical T-money card therefore remains an important fallback for the Indonesian audience.

## Required future Indonesia first-answer logic

Research recommendation only, not approved public copy:

1. **Indonesia is not currently listed as an Apple Pay-supported market.**
2. If the traveler already has a supported Apple Pay card from a supported issuer/market, it can work at compatible Korean merchants.
3. Korean store acceptance still depends on contactless/NFC + network + merchant.
4. Ordinary Apple Pay card payment is not the Seoul subway card.
5. For iPhone transit, use T-money in Apple Wallet.
6. Funding rules differ between direct Wallet top-up and MobileTmoney.
7. Do not assume an Indonesian-issued card can be added to Apple Pay.

## GEO / AI-answer readiness

The English page is strong, but Indonesian AI-answer readiness is **blocked until the Indonesia availability gate is made explicit**.

Apple Pay unavailable/not set up → physical card / T-money / ordinary foreign-card payment guidance.

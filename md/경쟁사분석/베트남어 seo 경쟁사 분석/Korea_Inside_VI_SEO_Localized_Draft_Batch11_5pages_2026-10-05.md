# 0.2 Vietnam-specific payment finding

Vietnam is materially different from the Indonesia Apple Pay case.

Current Apple support lists **Vietnam as an Apple Pay-supported market**, with many participating Vietnamese banks/card issuers. Therefore:

> **A Vietnamese traveler who already has an eligible Vietnamese-issued card in Apple Wallet can potentially use that Apple Pay card at compatible Korean NFC merchants.**

No Korean Hyundai Card is required merely to use an already-supported Vietnamese Apple Pay card in Korea.

However:

- merchant NFC/contactless support still matters;
- the underlying card network still matters;
- Apple Pay store payment and T-money public-transport top-up are different flows;
- MobileTmoney's current international-user top-up route lists Mastercard / American Express / UnionPay / JCB;
- **Visa is not currently listed for that MobileTmoney foreign-card top-up route**.

This means a Vietnamese Visa card may work for ordinary Apple Pay purchases at a compatible Korean merchant while still failing for MobileTmoney top-up.

This is a useful Vietnam-specific decision layer.

# Strong SEO findings from Batch 11

## 1. Vietnam-issued card should be explicit in search-facing copy

For Vietnam, a generic title:
> `Can You Use Foreign Cards in Korea?`

is weaker than:
> **`Thẻ Việt Nam có dùng được ở Hàn Quốc không?`**

The body must still remain network/issuer based and avoid promising that every Vietnamese card works.

## 2. Card-decline SERP is already highly competitive

Seoul Window currently has a very strong 2026 direct competitor page.

Korea Inside's defensible advantage is:
> **diagnose by failure context before blaming the card.**

The sequence:
- tap
- staffed terminal
- kiosk
- online
- ATM
- transit

should be protected.

## 3. Korean online payments are changing quickly

NAVER passport verification is a major 2026 change.

Current official rule:
- passport issued outside Korea
- no Korean mobile number required for the passport-verification path
- NAVER Map booking/order/payment travel functions supported

Do not repeat old blanket advice that all Korean online payments require a Korean phone number.

## 4. ATM is a good Vietnamese long-tail opportunity

The exact query:
> `rút tiền ATM ở Hàn Quốc bằng thẻ Việt Nam`

is less saturated than broad payment queries.

Korea Inside can win by explaining:
- network logo
- issuer setting
- failed ATM recovery
- fees
- DCC
- airport fallback

The EXK network currently includes Vietnam, but this must remain a supporting fact, not a universal bank-card promise.

## 5. Apple Pay is much easier to localize for Vietnam than Indonesia

Vietnam is currently an Apple Pay-supported market with many participating issuers.

Therefore the English page's basic assumption:
> “If you already use Apple Pay at home...”

maps cleanly to Vietnam.

The critical Vietnam-specific nuance is **Visa**:
- Vietnamese Visa cards can be valid Apple Pay store cards,
- but MobileTmoney's current foreign-user top-up list does not include Visa.

This distinction is valuable search content.

## 6. Store Apple Pay and transit Apple Pay must stay separate

Vietnamese readers are likely to assume:
> If Apple Pay works in Korea, my card should tap the subway gate.

That is wrong.

Current structure should remain:
- store → eligible payment card in Apple Wallet
- transit → prepaid T-money in Apple Wallet

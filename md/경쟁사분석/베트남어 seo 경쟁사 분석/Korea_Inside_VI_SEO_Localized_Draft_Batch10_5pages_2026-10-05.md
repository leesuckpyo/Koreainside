## 2. Vietnam IDP applicability — resolved

This was a P0 issue in the Indonesia market, but **not in Vietnam** after current verification.

### Vietnam-issued IDP basis

Vietnam's official IDP format is issued under:

> **1968 Vienna Convention on Road Traffic**

### Korea law

Current Korean Road Traffic Act Article 96 recognizes:
- 1949 Geneva Convention IDP
- **1968 Vienna Convention IDP**
- other mutual-recognition arrangements

Korea Road Traffic Authority also states:
- Korea normally recognizes Geneva IDPs;
- since January 1, 2002, valid IDPs issued by Vienna Convention countries are also permitted in Korea.

VisitKorea likewise states Korea recognizes IDPs issued by Geneva- or Vienna-Convention member states.

### Vietnam market conclusion

A valid official Vietnam-issued IDP under the Vienna Convention can be recognized in Korea.

But this does **not** mean:
- a Vietnamese domestic licence alone is always enough;
- any unofficial “international driving license” is valid;
- every rental company applies identical document rules.

Travelers should still bring:
- original Vietnamese driving licence
- official IDP
- passport
- accepted credit card
and check the rental company rules before payment.

### Important distinction

`International Driving Permit (IDP)`  
≠ unofficial `International Driving License`.

# Strong SEO findings from Batch 10

## 1. Vietnam rental-car applicability is resolved, unlike Indonesia

This is an important market difference.

Vietnam officially issues IDPs under the **1968 Vienna Convention**.

Korean law recognizes valid IDPs under both:
- Geneva 1949
- Vienna 1968

Therefore the Vietnam page does not require a P0 legal hold.

But the traveler still needs:
- official IDP
- original domestic licence
- passport
- rental-company-specific qualification checks

## 2. Apps page has a real 2026 factual defect

The statement:
> `KORAIL and SRT remain separate systems`

is no longer current.

KORAIL+ launched August 3, 2026, and the former separate high-speed rail booking structure changed from September 1.

This is not a Vietnamese wording preference. It is a source-freshness issue.

A generic:
> `visa or K-ETA requirements`

- visa/entry status

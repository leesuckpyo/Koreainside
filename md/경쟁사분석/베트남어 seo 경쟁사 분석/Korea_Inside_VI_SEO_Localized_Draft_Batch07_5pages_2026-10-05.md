## 1. Three eSIM pages can coexist cleanly

Cannibalization can be avoided by locking three different questions:

- `esim.html` → **How do I use eSIM in Korea?**
- `best-esim-for-korea.html` → **Which travel eSIM is best for my data use?**
- `korea-esim-with-phone-number.html` → **Do I need a Korean 010 number, voice or SMS?**

This is a strong cluster, not duplication, if title/H1/intro remain distinct.

## 2. Vietnamese eSIM SERP has a real local-market layer

Unlike many travel topics, Vietnamese telecom context matters because users think about:
- keeping the Vietnamese number
- receiving OTP
- VinaPhone/VNPT roaming
- buying before departure
- price in VND
- eSIM compatibility

This means Vietnamese localization should explicitly use:
- `giữ số Việt Nam`
- `roaming`
- `số 010`
where the English meaning already supports it.

Do not add unsupported Vietnam-carrier facts into unrelated English-derived body sections.

## 3. Best-eSIM freshness must be maintained more aggressively

Provider terms move faster than neighborhood content.

The Saily activation conflict proves that:
- comparison tables need regular first-party rechecks,
- provider help pages may conflict with terms/product pages,
- exact deadlines should not be frozen without recording a current source.

Current Vietnamese transaction SERP already recognizes:
- SKT

Korea Inside should exploit the **verification distinction**, not compete only on price.

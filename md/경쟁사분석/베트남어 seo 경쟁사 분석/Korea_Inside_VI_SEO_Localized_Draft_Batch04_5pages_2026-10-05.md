# Strong SEO findings from Batch 04

## 1. Traveler-purpose Stay pages are genuine independent intents

The general Stay Hub should not swallow:
- solo
- couples
- budget
- shopping
- nightlife

Each solves a different decision problem.

## 2. Exact Vietnamese long-tail is often less saturated than the broad hotel SERP

This is especially true for:
- solo
- couples
- area-level budget stay
- shopping-focused accommodation
- nightlife-focused accommodation

Korea Inside should not abandon these pages just because OTA pages dominate generic hotel terms.

## 3. Budget query needs strict query-role protection

`khách sạn Seoul giá rẻ` is strongly transactional.

Korea Inside should own:
> **total trip cost + area choice**

not:
> **cheapest current room inventory**

## 4. Shopping Stay should remain a hotel-base decision

Vietnam already has strong shopping guides.

Korea Inside's added value is:
> what fills the bags → how often you return → how you carry it back → airport departure

## 5. Nightlife is highly freshness-sensitive

Current Vietnamese editorial competitors are already updating:
- venue status
- cover charge
- opening days
- club economics
- entry rules

Korea Inside Stay content should therefore keep venue volatility outside the core evergreen area judgment unless it can be continuously revalidated.

## 6. Solo “safety” should be handled carefully

Vietnamese users search for safety, but broad neighborhood safety rankings are too crude.

Korea Inside should answer with actionable friction:
- well-understood route
- correct station exit
- final walk
- late return
- transport fallback

rather than unsupported “safest district” claims.

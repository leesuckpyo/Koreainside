# 0.2 Vietnam entry-applicability finding — critical

## Current first-party evidence

The Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Vietnam currently publishes:
- 2026 tourist visa guidance for `C-3-9`;
- visa-procedure guidance showing that ordinary Vietnamese-passport tourism is not part of the broad visa-free list;
- visa-free exceptions include specific cases such as diplomatic/service passports and APEC-card conditions.

The official e-Arrival Card navigator currently states:
- valid **visa holders are subject to e-Arrival Card completion**;
- valid K-ETA holders are treated differently;
- the e-Arrival Card is free and may be submitted within 3 days before arrival.

The official K-ETA site states K-ETA is for travelers who can enter Korea **without a visa**.

## Vietnam localization consequence

The current English `arrival.html` correctly explains K-ETA and e-Arrival Card in a global way, but its K-ETA temporary-exemption paragraph is **not the primary path for an ordinary Vietnamese passport holder traveling as a tourist**.

A Vietnamese translation that reproduces the K-ETA exemption discussion without a Vietnam-specific visa gate can create the wrong first impression.

### Research judgment

`arrival.html` = **P0 — VIETNAM ENTRY-ELIGIBILITY / APPLICABILITY GATE**

Before Vietnamese Content Lock:
- ordinary Vietnamese-passport tourist applicability must be stated correctly;
- K-ETA must not be presented as an alternative to the ordinary tourist visa path;
- special passport/APEC/group-tour exceptions must not be overgeneralized;
- e-Arrival Card requirements must be tied to actual entry status;
- final wording should use the newest Korean Embassy in Vietnam + official e-Arrival Card guidance.

This is a market-specific legal/entry applicability issue, not a stylistic localization preference.

# Strong SEO findings from Batch 08

## 1. Vietnamese arrival search is much more nationality-sensitive than transport search

For Vietnamese users:
- visa applicability
- e-Arrival Card
- K-ETA distinction

must be resolved before the page can be safely content-locked.

This is the first Vietnam P0 because the global English entry explanation cannot simply be treated as the primary Vietnamese travel path.

## 2. Vietnamese Incheon → Seoul SERP is now mature

Current competitors already provide:
- comparison tables
- fares
- 37 bus routes
- night buses
- AREX

Korea Inside cannot win by adding more rows.

It should win on:
- hotel door
- luggage
- final walk
- station transfer
- arrival-hall exit time
- family/group trade-off

## 3. Fare freshness is a real SEO moat

Some Vietnamese commercial pages still show:
- old AREX standard fare
or
- a discounted booking price as if it were the standard fare.

Current checked standard Express adult selling fare is **₩13,000**.

Korea Inside should keep:
- official fare
- commercial discount
- child/member fare

as separate concepts.

## 4. Airport bus rules changed materially in 2026

Airport Limousine's official rule from March 5, 2026:
- advance ticket purchase at Incheon Airport for its relevant buses;
- Terminal 2 uses B1 ticket/boarding flow.

Old advice saying “just tap T-money on board” should not be generalized across operators.

## 5. Late arrival must be calculated from terminal exit

The Incheon Airport official late-night network is substantial:
- N6000
- N6002
- N6701
- N6703

but a flight landing at 23:00 does not mean the passenger is ready to board at 23:00.

Immigration/baggage/customs remain part of the transfer decision.

## 6. Arrival Hall and Arrival/Immigration must stay separate

`arrival.html`:
- before public arrival hall

`airport.html`:
- after public arrival hall

This separation is good UX and good SEO when the Vietnamese titles make the distinction explicit.

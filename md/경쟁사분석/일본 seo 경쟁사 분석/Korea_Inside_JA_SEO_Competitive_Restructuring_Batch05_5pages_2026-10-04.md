# 0. Executive Summary

Batch 5 confirms the strength of the Korea Inside **Accommodation Decision Layer**.

Japanese competitors increasingly discuss:
- area
- price
- airport access
- nightlife
- shopping
- quietness
- hotel examples

But Korea Inside goes further:

> **trip purpose → area → actual station/exit → last walk → luggage → room/bed → late return/arrival → booking conditions → hotel → OTA**

This is the correct differentiation to protect.

## Final Batch 5 Classification

| Page | Final Status | Main Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Budget Travelers | **P1** | Search-facing alignment is strong. Improve total-cost visibility and selectively test secondary budget areas; do not turn the page into a live-price list. |

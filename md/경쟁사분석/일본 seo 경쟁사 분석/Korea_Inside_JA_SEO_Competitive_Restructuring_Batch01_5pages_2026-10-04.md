# 0. Executive Summary

The first Japanese 5-page batch shows that Korea Inside already has a strong **decision architecture**, but its Japanese search-facing layer is uneven.

Japanese competitors repeatedly win visibility through:

> **exact Japanese search wording → immediate answer → named entities → access/time/price → model course → FAQ/action**

Korea Inside is stronger at:

> **where to start → how long to stay → what to skip → who it suits → friction → weather → timing → next area → stay decision**

The correct restructuring strategy is therefore:

> **Do not replace Korea Inside with Japanese listicle SEO. Keep the decision-first body, but strengthen Japanese search wording, extractable answers, entity visibility and freshness at the surface.**

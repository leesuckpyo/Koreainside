# 0. Executive Summary

Batch 4 is the first pure **Stay Decision / Comparison** batch in the Japanese SEO program.

The central finding is clear:

> **Korea Inside is already structurally ahead of most Japanese stay-comparison competitors because it does not stop at “which area is popular.” It evaluates luggage, station exits, final walking distance, room size, beds, noise, arrival/return friction and the actual shape of the traveler’s day.**

Japanese competitors generally win with:

> **先に結論 → エリア比較 → メリット/デメリット → おすすめホテル → OTA**

Korea Inside’s stronger model is:

> **旅行目的 → エリア選択 → 実際の移動摩擦 → 客室条件 → ホテル候補 → OTA**

Therefore, four pages should mostly be protected.

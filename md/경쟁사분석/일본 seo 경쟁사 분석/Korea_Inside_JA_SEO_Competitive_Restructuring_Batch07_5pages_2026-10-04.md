# 7. Cross-Page Findings

## 7.1 The Japanese Hotel SERP Is Moving Toward Subarea Logic

The strongest current pages increasingly divide:
- Jamsil by purpose
- Gangnam by cluster
- Dongdaemun by station
- Seongsu by station/fallback
- Itaewon by nightlife/quiet/luxury side

This is exactly the correct architecture for Korea Inside.

## 7.2 “Should I Stay Here at All?” Is a Valuable SEO Question

Especially for:
- Seongsu
- Jamsil
- Itaewon

The answer can be:
> no, stay elsewhere and visit.

That is more useful than blindly monetizing every area as a hotel destination.

## 7.3 Event-Based Stay Needs a Two-Moment Rule

A special event should justify hotel relocation only if the new location helps at least two of:
- before event
- after event
- recovery/rest
- next morning

This is especially useful for:
- Lotte World
- concert
- baseball
- popups

## 7.4 Fallback Hotel Markets Can Be Better Than Forcing a Thin Local Market

Seongsu is the clearest example.

When local supply is thin:
- nearby better-supplied station
- same line
- better room options

can solve the problem more honestly than adding low-quality local inventory.

## 7.5 Exact Hotel Role Is More Important Than Hotel Count

Examples:
- Lotte Hotel World = park convenience
- Sofitel = lake/upper-upscale stay
- SIGNIEL = hotel experience
- POCO = Seongsu proximity
- Grand InterContinental = COEX
- Hamilton = central Itaewon/nightlife
- Grand Hyatt = hotel experience/hill trade-off

This is more useful than Top 15 lists.

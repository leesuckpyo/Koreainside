## 3. Core editorial judgments preserved

- Itaewon is optional on a short first trip focused on palaces and traditional Seoul.
- Hannam / Hangangjin is the stronger daytime start for art, design, shopping and fixed appointments.
- Leeum should take time only when art / architecture / design genuinely matters.
- Central Itaewon is strongest for international food, Muslim-friendly infrastructure and the main nightlife concentration.
- Gyeongnidan is the calmer evening extension.
- Haebangchon is worth the climb only when the hillside neighborhood itself is part of the experience.
- A full day makes sense only when several parts of the district genuinely interest the traveler.
- Staying in Itaewon should depend on repeated evening use, hills, noise, station distance and final walking friction—not the district name alone.

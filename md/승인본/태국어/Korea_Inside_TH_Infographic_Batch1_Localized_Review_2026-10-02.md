- Thai wording is concise enough for infographic use and avoids word-for-word English syntax.
- `ครับ/ค่ะ/นะคะ/นะครับ` are not used.
- Arabic numerals and source numbering are preserved.
- Brands such as `AREX`, `KTX`, `Lotte World`, and the illustrated `G` remain unchanged where required.
- Source `RETAIN` for place/entity labels means the entity identity is locked; Thai display forms below are the proposed localized labels for user-facing Thai artwork.
- `SEOUL STATION` remains exact in INF-003 because the source extraction classifies that illustrated station sign as RETAIN.
- Existing Thai assets for INF-001~003 are not assumed correct merely because files exist. After wording approval, Codex must compare their visible text against this approved mapping before deciding whether to preserve or rebuild them.
- INF-004~005 have no existing Thai target asset/page and must not trigger creation of unrelated Thai pages during infographic production.

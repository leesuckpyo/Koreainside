## 4. Editorial judgment preserved

- **Hongdae:** easiest all-round nightlife base when active evenings are frequent.
- **Itaewon:** international pubs/social nights.
- **Gangnam:** upscale evenings when south-Seoul daytime plans already overlap.
- **Myeongdong:** better when nightlife is occasional and daytime sightseeing matters more.
- **Mapo / Gongdeok:** access to western nightlife with quieter sleep and better airport handling.
- **Seoul Station:** transport/luggage first, nightlife atmosphere last.
- Exact street and final return route matter more than district name alone.

## 3. Core editorial judgments preserved

- Insadong usually does not need a full sightseeing day by itself.
- About 2 hours can work for a compact visit.
- About 4–5 hours makes sense when crafts, tea, galleries and thoughtful souvenir shopping are real priorities.
- First-trip historic-Seoul default:
  `Gyeongbokgung → Bukchon → Insadong → Jogyesa → one evening choice`
- Anguk is the easiest default for many first-time visitors, but not the only correct entry.
- Ssamzie-gil is worth seeing, but should not consume half the day by default.
- One traditional tea stop is usually enough.
- Jogyesa fits because it changes the pace without requiring a major detour.
- Ikseon-dong is the stronger continuation when the day should become cafés/restaurants/evening.
- Cheonggyecheon is stronger when the traveler wants to keep walking rather than enter more shops.
- Insadong is stronger in the daytime and early evening than as a nightlife base.

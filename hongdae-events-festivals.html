Hongdae Festivals & Events

Hongdae is more than a shopping district or nightlife stop. Its festivals bring independent music, books, street performance and neighborhood culture into the streets and venues around Hongik University Station, Yeonnam and nearby areas.

Check what is scheduled during your travel dates before changing your itinerary. If nothing matches your interests, the regular Hongdae walk still works. You can also look back at earlier festivals to see how this part of Seoul has used its streets, stages and cultural spaces.

Navigation: Upcoming & Planned Events · Choose by Interest · Past Festivals

Upcoming & Planned Events

Event schedules are not interchangeable with confirmed performances. A planned festival may change or be canceled, and a ticketed music event may sell out. Use the event organizer's latest announcement before setting aside time or money.

Choose a Hongdae Festival by Interest

Independent music and live performance

Hongdae's live-music events range from multi-venue club programs to open-air concerts. A festival ticket is worth considering when a particular artist, sound or atmosphere matters to you. Street performances are different: they can be part of a casual walk, but published busking slots can change.

Explore on this page: Live Club Day, Zandari Festa, Balladream Festival and MAPO ON STAGE.

Books, publishing and visual culture

Book festivals can make Hongdae feel very different from its evening shopping and music scene. Talks, readings, illustration exhibitions and street book markets reward visitors who are interested in the subject, not simply trying to collect another photo stop.

Explore on this page: Seoul Wow Book Festival editions from 2024, 2025 and 2026.

Streets and neighborhood celebrations

Some festivals focus on the district itself: its residents, artists, local streets or public spaces. These can be enjoyable even without a famous headline performer, especially when you want to experience the neighborhood beyond the normal shopping route.

Explore on this page: Red Road Festival, Red Road International Street Dance Festival and Yeonnam's community celebration.

Past Festivals

These are records of previous editions, not events you can attend now. A past date does not establish the date of the next edition. Historical entries help you understand what an event is about and decide whether to check for a future announcement.

MAPO ON STAGE — November 6, 2026

Status: PAST EVENT

Location: In front of Hongik University Station Exit 7, around Eoulmadang-ro 143 and 155

Purpose: Street-based music and K-pop programming in central Hongdae

The first MAPO ON STAGE combined K-pop activities and a large evening concert near Hongik University Station. Its announced daytime program included a K-pop arcade, and its evening schedule featured opening performances followed by invited artists. It brought the activity into a public street setting rather than asking visitors to travel to a separate concert arena.

What this tells future travelers: The festival was a one-day program, not a permanently running attraction. Check for a new announcement before expecting a similar event on another date.

Status: PAST EVENT

Location: Hongdae Red Road R1 and associated cultural spaces in Mapo

Theme: “Changing, Unchanging”

Organizer: WOW Culture Lab

The 22nd Seoul Wow Book Festival explored what changes in society and what remains important, using books and conversations as its starting point. Its published program connected street book stalls with discussions, readings, art and music-related activities at several venues. The experience depended on which sessions or exhibits interested you; not every program had the same venue or admission terms.

Who would have enjoyed it: Readers, publishing enthusiasts, and travelers who would welcome a slower cultural stop within a Hongdae day. Do not assume the pricing or program from 2026 applies to later editions.

Zandari Festa — September 4–6, 2026

Status: PAST EVENT

Location: Multiple Hongdae/Mapo live-music venues, including Musinsa Garage

2026 theme: “COLLABORATION”

Purpose: Showcase independent musicians and connect the live-music scene

Zandari Festa takes its name from an older local name associated with the area. Its 2026 edition used collaboration as a theme and spread the listening experience across several venues.

Who would have enjoyed it: Travelers interested in discovering unfamiliar artists or moving between small and mid-sized shows rather than attending one stadium-scale event. Its listed tickets were paid; the correct pass and access conditions depended on the selected product.

Purpose: Experience multiple independent music performances with a shared ticket

The 82nd Live Club Day connected several Hongdae live venues in one August evening. Instead of presenting one continuous concert, the format let ticket-holders choose which performances and stages to attend. The edition shows why Hongdae's music culture is not confined to one street or club.

Who would have enjoyed it: Travelers who already listen to independent music and are comfortable planning their own route between venues. The 82nd edition does not imply that another edition runs on every Friday.

How to Use This Guide Without Overplanning

First, find your travel dates. Then ask whether the event's main subject actually interests you: music, books, dance, or a neighborhood gathering. A festival that does not match your interests should not replace a good Hongdae afternoon just because it is happening nearby.

For events spread across multiple streets or venues, confirm the precise entrance and whether the program is free, ticketed or requires advance registration. For a visit to Hongdae without an event, start with the regular neighborhood route and keep your evening flexible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Hongdae festivals always free?

No. Public street programs and neighborhood events may be free, but independent-music showcases and individual book-festival programs may have ticket or reservation requirements. Check the particular edition and program before you go.

Does a festival from a previous year happen on the same dates next year?

Not necessarily. An archive date only describes that edition. Use a new organizer announcement to verify the next event's date, location and program.

Should I change my whole Hongdae itinerary for an event?

Only if the event is a reason you genuinely want to visit. An evening concert may replace other nightlife; a book festival may replace part of a shopping afternoon. The usual Hongdae route still works when no event interests you.

Does PLANNED mean the Red Road Countdown is confirmed?

No. The Seoul Festival Portal has a planned listing, but travelers should check a new announcement before making fixed arrangements around it.

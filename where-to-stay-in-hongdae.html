Where to Stay in Hongdae: Areas, Hotels and Real Location Guide

Hongdae works best when the neighborhood itself is part of your trip, not just a place to sleep.

It is one of Seoul’s easiest areas for the all-stop AREX from Incheon Airport, and the streets around Hongik University stay active long after most sightseeing days are over. The trade-off is that “near Hongik University Station” does not always mean an easy walk with luggage, and a room close to the busiest streets can feel very different from one near Yeonnam or toward Hapjeong.

This guide starts with the map, then narrows the choice by the route you will actually use.

Where is Hongdae in Seoul?

Hongdae sits west of central Seoul, around Hongik University and Hongik University Station. That position explains two of its biggest strengths: Incheon Airport is on the same side of the city, and the all-stop AREX serves Hongik University Station directly.

The location also explains the trade-off. If most of your days are built around Gyeongbokgung, Jongno, Myeongdong or other central sights, you will travel east more often. That is not difficult, but it becomes part of the daily routine.

For a first-time visitor, the useful question is not simply whether Hongdae is “central.” It is whether you would rather have cafés, restaurants and late evenings close to your hotel, or shorten more of your daytime sightseeing trips.

Hongdae is not one single block

A hotel can be listed as “Hongdae” and still give you a very different stay depending on which side of the neighborhood it sits on.

Around Hongik University Station and the main shopping streets, you are closest to the busiest part of Hongdae. That is useful if you want to walk back after dinner, shopping or a late night, but street activity can continue well into the evening.

The Yeonnam side feels different. You are still close to Hongdae, but cafés, smaller streets and the Gyeongui Line Forest Park shape more of the immediate surroundings. It can be a better fit if you want Hongdae nearby without putting the busiest streets directly outside the hotel.

Toward Hapjeong and the quieter edges of Seogyo, the trade changes again. You give up some station immediacy, but larger rooms, apartment-style stays or a calmer night can become easier to find.

That is why we do not rank every Hongdae hotel by one distance number.

A short distance on the map can still feel long with luggage

Hongik University Station is large enough that the final part of the journey matters.

A hotel may look close to the station pin while the route from the AREX platform still includes long corridors, level changes, elevator waits, crossings and a final walk outside. With a backpack, that difference can feel minor. With two large suitcases after a long flight, it may be the part of the journey you remember most.

For that reason, Korea Inside looks at the route in the same order a traveler uses it: airport transport, station movement, exit, street crossing and hotel entrance.

The map is not here to prove that the closest hotel is always the best one. It is here to show what you are trading.

What it’s like to stay in Hongdae

The strongest reason to stay in Hongdae is what happens after the main sightseeing day is over.

You can come back to the neighborhood, eat late, stop at a café, browse shops or keep walking without planning another trip across Seoul. For travelers who already expect to spend several evenings here, that is a real convenience rather than a lifestyle slogan.

The same energy is also Hongdae’s obvious weakness. A hotel near busy nightlife streets can be a poor choice for a light sleeper even when the location looks perfect on paper. Moving a few blocks away can change the night without making the neighborhood inconvenient.

Hongdae is also not only for clubbing or travelers in their twenties. Couples, families and repeat visitors use the area for very different reasons. The question is how much of your own trip happens here, and which side of the neighborhood supports it.

Airport access is a real advantage — but the last walk still matters

One of Hongdae’s biggest practical advantages is that Incheon Airport has two straightforward ways into the neighborhood. The all-stop AREX serves Hongik University Station directly, and Airport Limousine Bus 6002 also stops in Hongdae.

That matters most when you are carrying luggage. AREX avoids a rail transfer, while the airport bus can be easier when its stop is close to your hotel and you would rather avoid moving through a large station.

If airport convenience is one of the main reasons you are choosing Hongdae, give extra weight to the hotels that reduce that final movement.

If you are staying farther toward Hapjeong or in a smaller guesthouse, the trade can still be worthwhile — but you should treat the last part of the journey as a separate decision.

The trade-off is daytime travel into central Seoul

Hongdae is well connected, but it is not beside Seoul’s main palace and central sightseeing cluster.

If your itinerary repeatedly returns to Gyeongbokgung, Jongno, Myeongdong and nearby central areas, those trips add up over several days. For many travelers the extra movement is completely reasonable because they value Hongdae more in the evening.

For a short first trip built almost entirely around central sightseeing, however, Myeongdong can still be the simpler base.

The point is not that one area is better. It is to avoid choosing Hongdae for its reputation and then spending most of the trip somewhere else.

If you are still deciding between the two areas, compare Hongdae and Myeongdong before choosing a hotel.

How we narrowed the Hongdae stay list

There are far more Hongdae listings than a traveler needs to compare.

We did not build this list by taking the highest review scores, the cheapest nightly rates or the hotels closest to one map pin. Those are useful filters on an OTA, but they do not answer the question this page is trying to solve.

The stays below were kept because they represent different ways of using Hongdae: easier airport arrival, better access to the main streets, stronger value, more room, a quieter edge, or a guesthouse that works better for a lower budget or longer stay.

The categories are not a ranking. A cheaper hotel can be a better choice than a more expensive one if its location fits the trip better.

Hongdae has enough places to stay that a longer hotel list would not make the decision easier.

The stays here are included for different reasons. Some make the airport journey easier. Some keep you closer to the busiest streets. Others trade a little convenience for more room, a quieter night or better value.

These stays are not a ranking. Start with the part of the trip that matters most, then compare the options that solve that problem.

Hongdae signature stays

These are the stays to look at first when the hotel itself is part of why you chose Hongdae. Both keep the main streets close, but they offer a different kind of stay: L7 leans toward an easy, polished Hongdae base, while RYSE puts more weight on design and the room experience.

L7 HONGDAE by LOTTE HOTELS

L7 HONGDAE stayed on our list because it is one of the easiest all-round Hongdae choices. The main streets, cafés and shopping are close, while the hotel still feels like a proper stay rather than somewhere chosen only for price or airport access.

From Incheon Airport, you can take the all-stop AREX to Hongik University Station and walk to the hotel. Airport Limousine 6002 is another option in Hongdae, which can be useful when you have larger luggage and would rather avoid moving through a large station. For everyday travel, Line 2 makes it straightforward to head toward Euljiro, Seongsu and the Gangnam side of Seoul.

The compromise is the location itself. You are close to one of Hongdae’s busiest areas, so travelers who put a very quiet night above convenience may prefer a stay farther from the main streets.

RYSE, Autograph Collection

RYSE is here for travelers who want the hotel to be part of the Hongdae experience, not simply a room between sightseeing days. Design and the room experience carry more weight here, which makes it especially relevant for couples and travelers who expect to spend some real time at the hotel.

From Incheon Airport, the all-stop AREX takes you to Hongik University Station, followed by the final walk to RYSE. Airport Limousine 6002 also serves the Seogyo side of Hongdae, giving travelers with heavier luggage another practical way to arrive. Hongik University Station and Line 2 handle most everyday journeys around Seoul.

The trade-off is price, and you are still staying in the active center of Hongdae. If design matters less than a quieter night, another hotel on this list may fit the trip better.

Airport & AREX convenience

Hongdae’s airport advantage comes from the all-stop AREX, but that advantage is strongest when the accommodation actually works with the station. These two keep airport arrival and departure near the top of the decision.

Holiday Inn Express Seoul Hongdae

Holiday Inn Express Seoul Hongdae is on this list for one very practical reason: it makes the airport side of a Hongdae stay unusually simple. If you are arriving with luggage, leaving early for the airport or using the AREX more than once, this is one of the first hotels worth comparing.

From Incheon Airport, take the all-stop AREX directly to Hongik University Station. There is no rail transfer, and the final movement to the hotel is shorter and simpler than it is for many Hongdae stays. Once you are settled, Line 2 gives you direct access toward Euljiro, Seongsu and Gangnam.

The hotel is more about convenience than creating a distinctive Hongdae hotel experience. Travelers choosing Hongdae mainly for nightlife, design or a quieter neighborhood feel may find another option more interesting.

Expedia Booking.com Agoda

Mercure Ambassador Seoul Hongdae

Mercure Ambassador Seoul Hongdae made the list because it combines three things unusually well: airport access, Hongdae’s main streets and a more complete hotel stay. It is the stronger balance for travelers who want the practical side of Hongdae without choosing a hotel only because it sits beside the AREX.

From Incheon Airport, the all-stop AREX brings you directly to Hongik University Station, and Airport Limousine 6002 is another practical option into Hongdae. The hotel is also close to the main commercial streets, while Line 2 handles most cross-city travel once you are in Seoul.

That central position is also the drawback. Hongdae stays active late, so travelers who are particularly sensitive to street activity should pay attention to the room they book rather than assuming that the strongest location will also give them the quietest night.

Expedia Booking.com Agoda

Best value

Value in Hongdae is not the same as finding the lowest nightly rate. These two are here because location, room experience and price can make sense together without pushing too hard in only one direction.

9 Brick Hotel

9 Brick Hotel is our value choice for travelers who want to stay close to central Hongdae without moving straight into the higher price range of the better-known hotels. Its location keeps dinner, shopping and late evenings easy to reach on foot, which is why price alone is not the reason it made the list.

From Incheon Airport, the simplest rail route is the all-stop AREX to Hongik University Station, followed by the final walk to the hotel. Line 2 is nearby for everyday sightseeing trips across Seoul.

The trade-off is predictable for this part of Hongdae: a convenient position near the main activity can also mean more nighttime energy around the hotel. Light sleepers should compare the room conditions as carefully as the rate.

Expedia Booking.com Agoda

Junibino Hotel Hongdae

Junibino Hotel Hongdae is here because it gives travelers a different way to use Hongdae. Instead of staying beside Hongik University Station or directly in the busiest streets, you move toward Hapjeong and gain a calmer base while keeping Hongdae close.

From Incheon Airport, Airport Limousine 6002 serves Hapjeong, which can be useful with luggage. By rail, another option is the all-stop AREX to Hongik University Station and then continuing one stop on Line 2 to Hapjeong. Once there, Hapjeong Station gives you both Line 2 and Line 6 for travel around Seoul.

The compromise is that Hongdae’s main streets and the AREX are no longer immediately outside the hotel. Travelers who want the shortest possible airport walk should stay closer to Hongik University Station.

Expedia Booking.com Agoda

Quieter nights & more room

Not every Hongdae stay needs to put the busiest street outside the door. This group is for travelers who are willing to give up a little station immediacy for more room or a calmer end to the day.

Amanti Hotel Seoul

Amanti stayed on the list because a Hongdae trip does not always need the busiest streets outside the door. It gives travelers who want more breathing room and a calmer end to the day an alternative without moving the stay out of the Hongdae area.

From Incheon Airport, both the all-stop AREX to Hongik University Station and Airport Limousine 6002 can bring you into Hongdae, followed by the final walk to the hotel. Line 2 remains the main rail connection for sightseeing elsewhere in Seoul.

You give up some station immediacy in return. With heavy luggage, that last part of the journey matters more here than it does at Holiday Inn Express or the hotels closest to Hongik University Station.

Expedia Booking.com Agoda

Localstitch Creator Town Seogyo

Localstitch Creator Town Seogyo is on this list because it solves a different problem from a conventional Hongdae hotel. It is more useful for a longer stay, a work-and-travel trip or anyone who values shared living and working space as much as the hotel room itself.

From Incheon Airport, the all-stop AREX gets you to Hongik University Station, but the final walk is noticeably longer than it is for the station-side hotels. Line 2 is still available from Hongik University Station when you need to travel across Seoul, but this is not the option to choose simply for the shortest transport route.

That extra distance is the trade. In return, the stay puts more emphasis on living space, shared facilities and spending more than just sleeping hours at the property.

Budget-friendly stays

Hongdae has cheaper rooms than these, but price alone was not enough for this list. These two can lower the room cost without pushing the stay so far away that getting around becomes the main inconvenience.

Batwo Stay - For foreigners only

Batwo Stay made the list because it is a genuinely low-budget Hongdae option without the usual combination of poor location and weak guest feedback. It suits younger adult travelers who mainly need a clean place to sleep and would rather keep more of the budget for the rest of the trip.

From Incheon Airport, take the all-stop AREX to Hongik University Station and continue on foot. There is no rail transfer, and Line 2 is available from the same station for everyday travel around Seoul.

The restrictions are important: Batwo is for guests aged 18–35 and children are not accepted. It is also a guesthouse rather than a full-service hotel. If either of those conditions matters to your trip, this is an easy one to rule out.

Booking.com Agoda

Hotel Baroato 2nd

Hotel Baroato 2nd is the other low-cost option we kept, but it serves a different traveler from Batwo. It is better suited to adults who want a simple private-room stay near Hongdae without paying for hotel facilities they are unlikely to use.

From Incheon Airport, the all-stop AREX takes you directly to Hongik University Station, followed by the walk to the property. The same station gives you Line 2 for most daily travel around Seoul.

This is a smaller accommodation with more limited service than a large hotel, so arrival arrangements and the current booking conditions matter. It is not a family option, and travelers arriving late should check the property’s current check-in instructions before booking.

Expedia Booking.com Agoda

Guesthouses & small stays

Not every useful Hongdae stay needs to be a hotel.

These two keep Hongik University Station and the neighborhood easy to use while offering a smaller, more personal kind of stay. Hongdae Style works well for families and travelers who want flexible room types, while TwoTwo House is better suited to travelers who want Yeonnam nearby and practical features for a longer stay.

Hongdae Style Guesthouse

Hongdae Style Guesthouse is the small-stay option to look at when you want Hongik University Station close without paying for a chain hotel.

The station is only a short walk away, and the mix of private rooms, family rooms, luggage storage and a shared kitchen makes it more flexible than a basic budget room. Unlike Batwo Stay, it also works for families. The trade-off is simple: this is still a guesthouse, not a full-service hotel.

Expedia Booking.com Agoda

TwoTwo House

TwoTwo House is the better fit when Yeonnam and a more residential rhythm matter as much as the main Hongdae streets.

Hongik University Station is still only a few minutes away, while laundry, a shared kitchen and private-room options make it practical for a longer stay. It is less about hotel service and more about having a workable base. If your priority is stepping straight into the busiest Hongdae nightlife, the central hotels above put you closer.

So where should you stay in Hongdae?

Start with the part of the trip that will be hardest to change after you book.

If that is airport arrival with luggage, stay close to the route you will actually use from Hongik University Station.

If it is late evenings, put yourself close enough to walk back without placing a light sleeper directly on the busiest street.

If you are traveling as a group or staying longer, a little more distance can be a fair trade for more space.

Hongdae has enough accommodation that there is rarely one hotel everyone should book. The better decision is the stay whose location removes the friction that matters most to your trip.

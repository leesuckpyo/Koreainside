Where to Stay in Gangnam

Gangnam works best when the south side of Seoul is already part of your plan. COEX events, business meetings, beauty appointments, Apgujeong shopping, late dinners around Sinnonhyeon, and several days in southern Seoul are all good reasons to stay here.

It is less convincing when most of your trip is north of the river. If your days are mainly Gyeongbokgung, Insadong, Myeongdong, Hongdae, and Seoul Station, staying in Gangnam means crossing Seoul again and again. The subway makes that possible; it does not make the travel disappear.

The bigger mistake is treating Gangnam as one hotel area. Gangnam Station and Sinnonhyeon, Samseong and COEX, Apgujeong and Cheongdam, and Sinsa and Dosan lead to very different days. Pick the part of Gangnam first. The hotel becomes much easier after that.

A quick way to choose

COEX is the reason you are here: Start around Samseong. Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas keeps the walk to COEX and the trade-center district to a minimum.

You want COEX without paying for a luxury hotel: Compare Shilla Stay Samsung and Hotel Peyto Samseong. The rooms and facilities are simpler, but you are still staying where your schedule actually is.

You will be out late around Sinnonhyeon: Ocloud makes more sense than commuting back from Samseong after dinner or drinks.

You want a bath and sauna after long days: Dormy Inn Seoul Gangnam has small standard rooms, but the communal bath, sauna, and laundry are the point.

You are traveling with three or four friends: Hotel Cappuccino has dedicated Triple and Quad rooms. Check the room itself rather than assuming a standard twin can take everyone.

Apgujeong, Cheongdam, beauty, and luxury shopping are the trip: Andaz Seoul Gangnam puts you on the right side of Gangnam from the start.

Sinsa and Dosan Park matter more than Gangnam Station: Look at voco Seoul Gangnam. It fits that part of the city better than a hotel beside COEX.

Which part of Gangnam should you stay in?

Gangnam Station, Sinnonhyeon, and Yeoksam

This is the part of Gangnam to look at when work, clinics, restaurants, bars, and late evenings matter more than COEX.

The three names are close on a map, but do not assume every hotel is beside Gangnam Station. Novotel and Dormy Inn are easier from Sinnonhyeon than from Gangnam Station itself. AC Hotel sits closer to Yeoksam.

That distinction matters after a long day. If your appointments are around Teheran-ro, staying near Yeoksam can save more time than booking a hotel with “Gangnam” in the name closer to another station.

Sinnonhyeon also has the stronger late-night feel. If you expect dinner to turn into drinks and a late return, walking back can be more valuable than shaving a few minutes off a daytime subway ride.

Samseong and COEX

Stay here when COEX, Starfield, the trade center, conventions, or Samseong business appointments are doing most of the work in your itinerary.

This area is easy to misunderstand. It is part of Gangnam, but it does not feel like Gangnam Station at night. You get convention halls, malls, offices, department stores, and large hotels rather than the same concentration of bars and late-night streets.

For an exhibition or conference, that can be exactly what you want. Walking from your room to the venue is a better use of time than riding across Gangnam twice a day.

Apgujeong and Cheongdam

This is the stronger base for luxury shopping, beauty appointments, galleries, fine dining, and time around Apgujeong Rodeo and Cheongdam.

It is not automatically the “best” part of Gangnam because it is expensive. If your meetings are at COEX or your nights are around Gangnam Station, you will spend time moving back and forth.

But if several appointments and reservations are already in Apgujeong and Cheongdam, staying here removes that friction.

Sinsa and Dosan

Sinsa sits between several versions of Gangnam.

Garosu-gil, Dosan Park, restaurants, beauty, and shopping are close enough to shape the day, while Gangnam Station and COEX are not the immediate center of the stay.

This can work especially well for couples or repeat visitors who already know they will spend time around Sinsa and Dosan rather than using the hotel only as a base for classic Seoul sightseeing.

Hotels around Gangnam Station, Sinnonhyeon, and Yeoksam

Novotel Ambassador Seoul Gangnam

Novotel is useful when you want a full-service hotel in this part of Gangnam rather than a compact room and a good location.

The basic rooms are roughly 26–28 m², and the hotel has the facilities people often expect from a larger property, including a pool and sauna. Some room categories can also work for three people with the correct occupancy and extra-bed conditions.

The location needs to be described properly. Despite the name, this is not a hotel directly outside Gangnam Station. Sinnonhyeon is the easier subway approach, while walking from Gangnam Station itself takes longer.

That matters if you plan every trip around Line 2. If your evenings are around Sinnonhyeon and your days are in southern Seoul, the location makes more sense.

Airport arrival is another reason to look at it. The 6703 limousine serves this hotel area, so you do not necessarily need to drag luggage through a subway transfer after a long flight.

Dormy Inn Seoul Gangnam

The room is not the reason to book Dormy Inn.

Standard rooms are compact, but after a day of meetings, shopping, or walking, the communal bath and sauna change the stay. There is also a laundry area, which makes it easier to pack lighter on a longer Korea trip.

The bath operates into the next morning; the sauna pauses for part of the overnight period. Anyone booking specifically for those facilities should check the current operating hours before arrival.

The hotel is also closer to Sinnonhyeon than many travelers expect from the name. The 6703 airport bus stop near Novotel leaves only a short walk to Dormy Inn.

If you want a large room to spread out two suitcases, look elsewhere. If you know you will actually use the bath every night, the small room becomes a more reasonable compromise.

Ocloud Hotel Gangnam

Ocloud is the practical choice in this group when Sinnonhyeon itself is useful to you.

The hotel is technically across the administrative boundary in Seocho-gu, but that distinction matters much less to a traveler than the actual location: it sits right by the Gangnam/Sinnonhyeon nightlife and dining area.

That makes it a sensible place to come back to after a late dinner without planning your evening around the last subway.

Family Twin categories add another use case, although occupancy listings have not always been consistent across booking sites. Do not count beds and assume the room accepts the same number of adults. Put the full party into the reservation first.

Ocloud is harder to justify for someone spending every day at COEX. In that case, staying in Samseong removes more travel.

Hotel Cappuccino

Cappuccino feels different from the more conventional business hotels nearby, but the useful part is not just the design.

It has dedicated Triple and Quad rooms, and the Quad uses four bunk-style beds. For three or four friends, that can solve a sleeping problem that standard Seoul hotel rooms often do not.

There is also self-service laundry, which is more valuable on a longer trip than another decorative hotel amenity.

The hotel suits travelers who like the Yeoksam/Gangnam evening environment and do not need a large full-service property. Someone looking for a quiet luxury stay or direct COEX access has better choices elsewhere.

For a group, still book by the exact room category. A stylish photograph of a standard room is not evidence that four adults can stay there.

AC Hotel Seoul Gangnam

AC Hotel works better as a Yeoksam hotel than as a generic “Gangnam Station hotel.”

Yeoksam Station is the useful reference point, particularly for business along Teheran-ro. That can cut a surprising amount of daily walking if your meetings are nearby.

Most travelers will use it as a modern conventional hotel, but some higher room categories go further. Premier Suite options include a separate living area and full kitchen, which changes the usefulness for longer stays.

That does not mean everyone should pay for a suite. If you will leave after breakfast and return late, the kitchen may barely matter.

AC Hotel is strongest when Yeoksam is already where you need to be.

Hotels around Samseong and COEX

Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas

For a COEX-heavy trip, this is the hotel that removes the most friction.

Classic rooms start around 40 m², so even the basic room gives you noticeably more space than many business hotels in Seoul. The hotel also connects into the Parnas Mall/COEX area, and Samseong Station is very close.

That changes the morning of a conference. Instead of planning when to leave, watching the weather, and carrying work material on the subway, you can simply walk over.

The 6103 airport limousine also serves this area, which makes arrival with luggage easier than a rail trip with transfers.

The price only makes sense if you use the location or the hotel. If COEX is one short visit during a week of sightseeing elsewhere, paying for this convenience every night is harder to defend.

Shilla Stay Samsung

Shilla Stay is for travelers who need Samseong more than they need a five-star hotel.

Family Twin categories can accommodate three guests, with some child combinations allowing four, so it is worth checking for a family attending an event or spending time around COEX.

There is one detail easy to miss when booking: some Family Twin and standard categories are sold as no-view rooms. A family room does not automatically mean a better view or a brighter room.

If looking out over Gangnam matters to you, check the exact category rather than assuming the hotel name guarantees it.

For a conference, shopping, or short family stay around COEX, the simpler hotel facilities may be enough.

Hotel Peyto Samseong

Peyto is here for travelers who look at the COEX map and think, “I mostly need a clean place to sleep nearby.”

It sits close to COEX and has practical facilities including coin laundry. That makes it particularly useful for one or two travelers who care more about location than spending the evening inside the hotel.

Do not treat the twin-room layout as flexible group accommodation. Extra-bed options are limited, so this is not where we would start for three adults.

It also fits the shopping side of Gangnam well. If COEX, department stores, and Samseong are the point of the day, staying nearby is more useful than commuting from Gangnam Station simply because that area is more famous.

Apgujeong and Cheongdam

Andaz Seoul Gangnam

Andaz is the strongest hotel on this list when the trip is actually about Apgujeong and Cheongdam.

The hotel connects directly with Apgujeong Station, and that puts luxury shopping, beauty appointments, galleries, restaurants, and the Cheongdam side of Gangnam in the right direction from the moment you leave.

Rooms start larger than many central Seoul hotels, and higher categories give substantially more space. Some configurations can also work for families, but the exact occupancy still needs to be checked at booking.

This is not the hotel we would pay for simply to say we stayed in Gangnam. If your days are at COEX, Andaz creates a commute that Grand InterContinental does not.

If your calendar is filled with Apgujeong clinics, Cheongdam reservations, and shopping, the calculation reverses.

Sinsa and Dosan

voco Seoul Gangnam

voco makes the most sense when Sinsa and Dosan are already on your map.

Standard rooms are not unusually large, but the hotel has much bigger options for groups and longer stays. The Two Bedroom Royal Suite is over 100 m² and includes multiple beds and a full kitchen.

Most couples do not need anything close to that. The useful point is that the same property can work for a normal two-person stay or, with the correct category, a much larger family or group.

There is no hotel airport shuttle to rely on, so check the airport limousine or final taxi leg before arrival rather than assuming a premium hotel handles the transfer.

For Garosu-gil, Dosan Park, beauty appointments, and dinners around Sinsa, the location earns its place. For a COEX conference, it does not.

Getting to Gangnam from Incheon Airport

There is no single “Gangnam airport bus” that works equally well for every hotel on this page.

Gangnam, Sinnonhyeon, and Yeoksam

The 6703 limousine is useful for the Novotel/Dormy Inn side.

For Dormy Inn, getting off near Novotel leaves only a short final walk. That is very different from arriving by airport rail and transferring with luggage.

For AC Hotel and other Yeoksam stays, check where the bus actually leaves you before assuming the same stop is convenient.

Samseong and COEX

The 6103 limousine is the important route here.

For a COEX or Grand InterContinental stay, it removes the need to cross Seoul by subway with suitcases. Hotel Peyto and Shilla Stay still require you to check the final walk from the stop.

“Near COEX” does not mean every hotel entrance is beside the same bus stop.

Apgujeong and Sinsa

Andaz and voco use a different arrival pattern again.

Andaz can be reached using airport-bus options serving the Apgujeong area, but the final walk still matters with luggage. For Sinsa and Dosan, compare the available limousine stop with a taxi for the last section rather than choosing by bus number alone.

Save the hotel’s Korean address before you fly. That becomes useful if you decide the final ten or fifteen minutes are easier by taxi.

Before you book

Start with the reason you chose Gangnam.

If it is COEX, do not let a famous Gangnam Station hotel pull you away from Samseong. If it is nightlife, do not book Samseong just because the hotel is luxurious. If it is an Apgujeong clinic or shopping itinerary, check how often you would actually need Line 2.

Then check the room itself.

A family room may have no view. A twin room may not accept three adults. A large suite may still have a lower occupancy limit than the floor area suggests. A hotel with a kitchen may only provide it in expensive suite categories.

Enter every adult and child before comparing prices. Save the exact room name and bed configuration.

For a multi-night stay, also ask what you will actually use. A pool, lounge, sauna, kitchen, and coin laundry have value only if they change how your trip works.

Frequently asked questions

Is Gangnam a good place to stay for a first trip to Seoul?

Only when a meaningful part of the trip is already in southern Seoul.

COEX, business, clinics, Apgujeong shopping, Gangnam dining, and appointments can make Gangnam a very practical base. If most sightseeing is around the palaces, Insadong, Myeongdong, Hongdae, and Seoul Station, staying farther north or west usually cuts daily travel.

Should I stay near Gangnam Station or COEX?

Follow your itinerary.

Gangnam Station and Sinnonhyeon are better for restaurants, nightlife, clinics, and parts of the business district. Samseong is better for COEX, conventions, trade-center business, and shopping around the mall.

They are both “Gangnam,” but they do not produce the same trip.

Where should I stay for shopping in Gangnam?

For COEX and department-store shopping, Samseong is easier; Hotel Peyto and the larger COEX hotels keep you close.

For luxury boutiques, Apgujeong and Cheongdam are the stronger area, and Andaz is better positioned for that itinerary.

Do not use the word “shopping” alone to choose the hotel. Decide which shopping district you mean.

Where should I stay for Gangnam nightlife?

Look first around Sinnonhyeon and the Gangnam/Yeoksam side.

Ocloud works particularly well when you expect late dinners or drinks around that area. Hotel Cappuccino gives groups another option with dedicated Triple and Quad rooms.

If nightlife is the point, staying at COEX can leave you commuting back after the evening you came to Gangnam for.

Which Gangnam area works best for families?

It depends on the family’s schedule.

For COEX, Shilla Stay Samsung has family-room configurations without requiring a luxury hotel. Novotel provides more full-service facilities around Sinnonhyeon, while larger voco suites can work for families or groups staying around Sinsa.

A family visiting mainly the palaces and central Seoul should first reconsider whether Gangnam is the right base at all.

Is Andaz or Grand InterContinental better?

They solve different problems.

Grand InterContinental is the more practical choice for COEX and Samseong. Andaz is better placed for Apgujeong, Cheongdam, luxury shopping, beauty, and dining.

The better hotel for your trip is the one that removes the journeys you would otherwise repeat.

Is Gangnam convenient from Incheon Airport?

It can be, if the airport bus matches your part of Gangnam.

6703 works well for the Sinnonhyeon/Novotel area, while 6103 is useful around COEX and Samseong. Apgujeong and Sinsa require their own final-leg check.

Do not compare hotels using “airport bus available” as a yes/no feature. Compare where the bus actually stops and what remains after you step off.

Is it worth paying more for a hotel connected to COEX?

For a multi-day conference, exhibition, or work schedule, often yes.

Walking to the venue can remove two daily subway trips and make it easy to return to the room between sessions.

For a tourist visiting COEX for an afternoon, that same convenience may not justify several nights of higher rates.

FIRST TRIP TO KOREA

Korea Travel Guides for Your First Trip

A first trip to Korea feels much easier when a few practical things are sorted out before you land. Knowing where you'll stay, how you'll get from the airport, how you'll get online, and what you'll use for transport and payments takes a lot of stress out of the first day.

WHAT BRINGS YOU TO KOREA?

Start with what you came for.

Some trips begin with food. Others are about shopping, beauty, nightlife, or simply spending time in a neighborhood you've wanted to see. Once you know what you're most excited about, the rest of the itinerary becomes easier to shape.

FOOD · TRAVEL GUIDE

Taste Korea

Korean food changes from neighborhood to neighborhood and city to city. A barbecue night in Seoul, a market morning in Busan, or a café day in Seongsu can lead to very different places—and sometimes a different place to stay.

BEFORE YOU GO

A few things are easier to sort out before you arrive

You do not need every detail planned. It helps to know where you're staying, how you'll get online, how you'll leave the airport, and what you'll use for transport and payments.

Accommodation

Where you stay shapes how much time you spend on trains, how easy it is to get back with shopping bags, and how tiring arrival day feels. A good base depends on what you plan to do most.

Transportation

The fastest ride from the airport is not always the easiest one. Luggage, arrival time, hotel location, and how far you have to walk after the station can matter more.

Payments

Cards are widely used in Korea, but cash, a transport card, or a backup payment method can still be useful. Knowing what you'll use before the first purchase saves a lot of small hassles.

HOW KOREA WORKS

A few things work differently once you arrive

Transport cards, payment habits, mobile data, and map apps may not work quite the way you expect at home. Knowing the differences ahead of time makes the first day in Korea much easier.

First hour

Your first hour in Korea

After baggage claim, most travelers need the same few things: a working phone, a way to pay, a map, and a clear route to the city. Once those are sorted, the airport becomes much easier to leave.

Airport Internet Transport
  1. Get connected to the internet
  2. Get or load T-money
  3. Keep some cash as backup
  4. Open Naver Map or KakaoMap
  5. Take AREX, an airport bus, taxi, or pickup to your hotel

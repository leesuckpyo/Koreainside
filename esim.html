Compatibility first

Check whether your phone will work

A compatible unlocked phone is the main requirement for using an eSIM in Korea. Complete these checks before comparing plans.

  1. Step 1
    Confirm eSIM support

    Check that your phone model can install an eSIM. On iPhone, the basic path is Settings → Cellular or Mobile Data → Add eSIM. On Android, it is usually Settings → Connections or Network → SIM manager → Add eSIM. Menu names vary by device and OS version.

  2. Step 2
    Confirm carrier-unlocked status

    Make sure your home carrier allows another mobile plan on the phone.

  3. Step 3
    Check the plan conditions

    Confirm that the included services and activation timing fit your arrival and travel needs.

  4. Step 4
    Save the installation details

    Keep the QR code, manual activation details, and provider instructions available before departure.

  • Check whether the plan is data-only.
  • Confirm hotspot, fair usage, speed limits, and refund rules.

Local mobile service

Need a Korean phone number?

A Korean-number eSIM or physical SIM may fit travelers who need local calls, SMS, delivery contact, hotel or driver calls, some reservations, or longer-stay support. This is different from a typical international data-only eSIM.

Korean eSIM and SIM plans

Use this option when local mobile services matter more than the fastest online setup. If you need a Korean number, domestic calls, or SMS, compare Korean carrier eSIMs with a phone number to see how SK Telecom, KT, and LG U+ differ in activation, calling, messaging, and identity-verification limits.

  • Check passport verification and activation timing.
  • Confirm calls, SMS, and Korean-number availability.
  • Compare airport pickup, store pickup, and online setup.

Some Korean telecom or tourist SIM/eSIM products may require passport verification, especially when the plan includes a Korean number, voice calls, or SMS. Before purchase, confirm the activation method, pickup method, passport requirement, and included services.

Setup sequence

Install and activate your eSIM

Use the provider's installation method and activation timing. Keep the setup information available until the connection is working.

  1. Step 1
    Keep the installation details ready

    Save the QR code, manual activation details, and provider instructions. Some eSIMs cannot be reinstalled with the same QR code after deletion.

  2. Step 2
    Follow the provider's setup method

    Install through the QR code, app, counter, or staff setup method provided with the plan. Complete passport verification if the selected product requires it.

  3. Step 3
    Activate at the correct time

    Follow the provider's instructions about when activation begins. After activation, select the eSIM as the mobile data line and turn on data roaming if the plan requires it.

After landing

Check your connection after landing

If the eSIM is installed but the connection does not work, check the line settings and activation status before deleting anything.

  • The eSIM is selected as the mobile data line.
  • Data roaming is turned on if the provider requires it.
  • The provider's activation period has started.
  • The internet connection works before you leave the airport or accommodation.
  • Your primary SIM remains active if you need messages from home.
  • Calls and SMS are included if you expect to use them.

Avoid common failures

Mistakes that cause the most trouble

Scanning an expired or used QR code

Why it causes trouble
The QR code may be expired, already used, or difficult to read.
What to do instead
Try the manual activation details or contact the provider before deleting anything.

Leaving the wrong data line selected

Why it causes trouble
The eSIM may be installed while mobile data still uses another line, data roaming is off, or activation has not started.
What to do instead
Check the eSIM line settings, roaming setting, activation timing, and provider instructions.

Expecting calls or SMS from a data-only plan

Why it causes trouble
A data-only plan does not include calls, SMS, or a Korean phone number unless stated.
What to do instead
Confirm every included service before purchase and complete number verification if required.

Buying while the phone is carrier-locked

Why it causes trouble
A locked phone may not accept another mobile plan.
What to do instead
Ask your home carrier about unlocking before you travel.

Deleting the eSIM too early

Why it causes trouble
Some eSIMs cannot be reinstalled with the same QR code.
What to do instead
Contact the provider and confirm whether reissue or reinstallation is possible before deleting it.

Important: Having a Korean phone number does not always mean you can complete identity verification on Korean apps.

Bottom line

Final recommendation

Most short-term visitors

Choose a data-only eSIM when a compatible unlocked phone mainly needs maps, messaging, translation, and browsing. It can be installed before arrival without physical pickup.

Travelers needing a Korean number

Choose a Korean-number eSIM or physical SIM, then confirm calls, SMS, passport requirements, and activation timing. Do not assume the number will support every Korean identity check.

Travelers with unsupported or locked phones

Consider a physical SIM if the phone does not support eSIM. If it is carrier-locked, ask the home carrier about unlocking before relying on another mobile plan.

Travelers who value maximum simplicity

International roaming keeps the original number active and avoids installing another SIM. Check the home carrier's price and plan limits because roaming is often more expensive.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use eSIM immediately after landing in Korea?

Usually yes if your phone supports eSIM, the plan is installed, and the provider allows activation after arrival. Some plans require data roaming to be turned on for the eSIM line.

Do I need passport verification?

Some Korean SIM or eSIM products may require passport verification, especially when the plan includes a Korean phone number, voice calls, or SMS.

Can I get a Korean phone number with eSIM?

Some Korean eSIM plans include a Korean phone number. Many international eSIM plans are data-only, so confirm phone number, calls, and SMS before buying.

Does a Korean phone number work for all Korean app verification?

No. A Korean phone number alone does not guarantee access to every Korean identity verification service. App rules and telecom registration types can differ.

Is Google Maps enough in Korea?

Google Maps can help, but Korea often requires local map apps for better walking, transit, store, and address information. Prepare Naver Map or KakaoMap as alternatives.

Should I buy before arrival or at the airport?

Buying before arrival can save time if your phone is compatible. Airport pickup may be better if you want staff help, a physical SIM, or a Korean number plan.

Can I keep my WhatsApp or LINE number?

Yes. WhatsApp and LINE usually keep using your existing account number as long as the app remains installed and you have mobile data or Wi-Fi.

What happens if I delete my eSIM?

Some eSIMs cannot be reinstalled after deletion. Do not delete an eSIM unless the provider confirms it can be reissued or reinstalled.

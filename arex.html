AREX TRAIN GUIDE

Incheon Airport to Seoul by AREX: Express vs All-Stop

Choose the Express Train for the fastest nonstop ride to Seoul Station and an assigned seat. Choose the All-Stop Train for Hongdae, Gongdeok, Gimpo Airport, a lower fare or a direct subway connection before Seoul Station.

For Myeongdong, Gangnam or another hotel beyond the AREX line, compare the transfer and final walk—not only the train time. Travelers with several large bags, children or a late arrival may find an airport limousine bus or an official taxi easier.

Choose Express

Your destination is Seoul Station or a simple connection from it, and speed plus an assigned seat matter more than the lowest fare.

Choose All-Stop

Your destination is Hongdae, Gongdeok or Gimpo Airport, or you want to use T-money and pay the lower distance-based fare.

SECTION 02

What Is AREX?

AREX is the airport railroad connecting Incheon Airport with central Seoul. Its two services solve different travel problems.

Express Train

The Express Train runs from Terminal 2 through Terminal 1 to Seoul Station without intermediate passenger stops. It uses a separate ticket and provides an assigned seat, making it the predictable option for a direct Seoul Station or KTX plan.

All-Stop Train

The All-Stop Train serves every AREX station, including Gimpo Airport, Hongik University and Gongdeok. It works like commuter rail, accepts T-money and often removes unnecessary backtracking through Seoul Station.

AREX matters because “Seoul” is not one destination. Hongdae, Gongdeok and Gimpo Airport are directly on the All-Stop route, while Seoul Station connects onward to Subway Lines 1 and 4 and KTX. The fastest train to Seoul Station is not always the fastest route to your hotel.

Decision rule

If the station-to-hotel route includes a difficult transfer, stairs or a long walk with luggage, compare all Incheon Airport transfer options. An airport bus can reduce station handling, while an official taxi can provide the simplest late-night or door-to-door backup.

SECTION 04

AREX Price Guide

Express uses a fixed selling fare between Seoul Station and either airport terminal. All-Stop uses a distance-based commuter fare, so T2 costs more than T1 for the same Seoul destination.

Express Train fare

AREX lists an adult selling fare of ₩13,000 and a child fare of ₩9,500. The same selling fare applies at T1 and T2 because Express uses a separate reserved ticket rather than a distance-based transit-card fare. The operator also displays higher normal fares, so check the official price for your travel date instead of relying on an old third-party ₩9,500 adult price.

All-Stop fare

All-Stop uses distance-based adult fares for prepaid and postpaid transit cards. From T1, the fare is ₩4,750 to Seoul Station and ₩4,650 to Hongik University; from T2, it is ₩5,350 and ₩5,250. T2 costs ₩600 more on both sample trips because it is farther along the route. Check the official fare table for any other destination.

T-money versus a single-use card

Use T-money by tapping in and out on the All-Stop Train; Express requires its own reserved ticket. If you buy an adult single-use transportation card instead, AREX adds ₩100 to the transit-card fare and collects a ₩500 refundable deposit. After you exit at your destination, insert the single-use card into a deposit refund machine to receive the ₩500 back.

Payment and booking check

The official booking screen lists ₩13,000 for a nonmember adult, ₩12,500 for a member and ₩9,500 for a child. After online payment, open the QR ticket on your phone or print the home ticket, then scan it directly at the Express gate without exchanging it at a machine. AREX lists Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners Club, American Express and UnionPay among supported foreign-issued cards, but does not guarantee approval by every card issuer. If payment is declined, try another supported card or buy the ticket at an Express ticket machine or customer center. For All-Stop, AREX station machines can sell a single-use transportation card by credit card; T-money is a separate option, so learn how to buy and use T-money before relying on it.

SECTION 05

AREX Time Guide

The official train time is not the same as the time from the aircraft door to your hotel door.

From Terminal 1

Express reaches Seoul Station in 43 minutes. All-Stop is scheduled for 59 minutes, with some services taking 2–6 minutes longer.

From Terminal 2

Express reaches Seoul Station in 51 minutes. All-Stop is scheduled for 66 minutes, with some services taking 2–6 minutes longer.

Add the walk from the arrival hall to the station, ticket purchase or card setup, waiting time, the transfer inside Seoul Station or another hub, and the final walk to the hotel. Express can save main-ride time but lose its advantage if you must backtrack from Seoul Station.

SECTION 08

How to Find AREX at Terminal 1 and Terminal 2

Both terminals have their own AREX station. Confirm your terminal and follow Airport Railroad signs instead of copying directions for the other building.

Do not rely on a saved gate or exit number. Airport circulation and temporary routes can change. Review the broader Incheon Airport arrival process before your flight if this is your first arrival.

SECTION 09

How to Buy a Ticket

Decide which train you need before approaching a machine or gate. Express and All-Stop do not use the same ticket.

Express online reservation

Reserve on the official AREX website or app by selecting the terminal, travel date, train and passengers. The official system accepts reservations up to 20 minutes before departure and up to 60 days ahead. Payment creates a ticket with a QR code, train, car and assigned seat.

Express at the station

Use an Express ticket machine or customer information center at T1, T2 or Seoul Station. Supported foreign-issued cards include major international networks listed by AREX. A station-issued Express ticket remains separate from T-money.

All-Stop single-use card

Choose the destination at an All-Stop ticket machine, pay the fare plus the deposit, keep the card through the trip and refund the deposit after exiting. Credit-card purchase is available at AREX station machines, but carrying a backup payment method is sensible.

All-Stop with T-money

Make sure the balance covers the trip, then tap at the All-Stop gate and tap out at the destination. Do not scan a T-money card at an Express-only gate expecting it to act as an Express ticket.

Changes and returns

The official booking site provides ticket change and return functions, and may show a fee before confirmation. Review the current conditions before paying and complete any change before the stated departure cutoff rather than relying on an old reseller rule.

SECTION 10

Seoul Station Transfers and What Happens Next

Reaching Seoul Station ends the airport train ride, but it does not end the trip for most travelers.

Subway Lines 1 and 4

Line 1 serves several central and eastern connections. Line 4 is the usual subway continuation toward Myeongdong and Dongdaemun-area stations. Follow signs for the correct line and direction rather than assuming every subway entrance leads to the same platform.

KTX and national rail

Do not build a tight KTX connection from the published AREX arrival minute. Include platform exit, elevators or escalators, ticket checks and movement through the station with luggage.

Myeongdong and nearby hotels

Continue on Line 4 or use a short surface connection that fits the exact hotel. The best choice depends on the exit, final walk and whether lifting luggage through another transfer is practical.

Station walking burden

AREX platforms are deep within a large complex. Allow extra time for elevators, corridors and finding the next service, especially with children, a stroller, limited mobility or several suitcases.

Use Korean map apps to verify the hotel pin, station exit and final walk. An area name alone is not enough to judge the transfer.

City Airport Terminal is for departure

The Seoul Station City Airport Terminal is not an arrival service. It is available to eligible same-day international passengers using the Express Train, and participating airlines vary. Check the official eligibility and airline list before planning check-in or baggage drop there.

SECTION 11

Luggage, Families and Accessibility

A reserved seat helps only after boarding. The difficult part may be reaching the platform and changing trains in Seoul.

Express

Assigned seating and dedicated luggage space make the main ride more predictable. You must still move every bag through the airport station, Seoul Station and any next connection.

All-Stop

It operates like a commuter train. A seat is not guaranteed, and crowding can make large bags, a stroller or supervising children more difficult.

Elevators and transfers

Check elevators at the arrival station and the route to the next line. Seoul Station is large, so build in extra walking time for a stroller, wheelchair or several suitcases.

When to change modes

Choose a verified airport bus stop or appropriately sized official taxi when repeated lifting, long corridors or the final outdoor walk creates more burden than the rail saving is worth.

SECTION 12

First Train, Last Train and Late-Night Arrivals

AREX does not run 24 hours. Use the official timetable for your terminal and date, then calculate from arrival-hall exit time—not landing time.

The official Express timetable lists the first airport departures at 05:16 from T2 and 05:24 from T1, and the last at 22:40 from T2 and 22:48 from T1. All-Stop departures follow a different timetable and can change, so check the official dated AREX timetable instead of copying a saved screenshot.

  1. Start with the scheduled landing time.
  2. Add immigration, baggage claim, customs and the walk to the station.
  3. Allow time to buy the correct ticket and reach the correct platform.
  4. Check the live T1 or T2 departure—not the other terminal.
  5. Save a backup that works after the time you can actually board.

If the last train no longer works

Check an official night bus or other airport bus option. If no suitable service remains, use an official airport taxi stand or a verified pickup with clear meeting and delay terms.

SECTION 13

Common AREX Mistakes

Most costly mistakes happen before the platform: choosing the wrong train, terminal, ticket or transfer plan.

  • Taking Express for a Hongdae hotel even though it does not stop at Hongik University.
  • Ignoring the transfer and final walk after arriving at Seoul Station.
  • Entering the Express area with an All-Stop plan, or the reverse.
  • Judging the last train from aircraft landing time.
  • Checking T1 directions or departures when the flight arrives at T2.
  • Trusting an old fare or timetable saved by another website.
  • Assuming T-money works on Express because it works on All-Stop.
  • Choosing rail automatically even when several large bags make a verified bus stop or taxi easier.
  • Buying from a third party without checking the official fare and change conditions.

SECTION 14

AREX Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions that most often change the train decision.

How much is AREX from Incheon Airport to Seoul?

The Express adult selling fare is ₩13,000. With a transit card, All-Stop to Seoul Station costs ₩4,750 from T1 or ₩5,350 from T2. Check the current official fare before travel.

How long does AREX take to Seoul Station?

Express takes 43 minutes from T1 or 51 minutes from T2. All-Stop takes 59 minutes from T1 or 66 minutes from T2, with some trains taking 2–6 minutes longer.

Is AREX Express faster than All-Stop?

Yes to Seoul Station. It is not automatically faster to a hotel near Hongdae, Gongdeok or another All-Stop station because Express may require backtracking.

Can I use T-money on AREX?

Use T-money on the All-Stop Train. Express requires a separate reserved ticket.

Which AREX train should I take to Hongdae?

Take the All-Stop Train directly to Hongik University Station. Express does not stop there.

Does AREX run 24 hours?

No. Check the official timetable for your terminal and travel date, especially after a late flight.

Where is AREX at Terminal 1?

After customs, follow Airport Railroad / AREX signs from the public arrival hall toward the Terminal 1 Transportation Center and rail area.

Where is AREX at Terminal 2?

After customs, follow Airport Railroad / AREX signs from the public arrival hall toward the Terminal 2 Transportation Center and rail area.

Is AREX good with large luggage?

Express has assigned seating and dedicated luggage space, but you still handle bags through stations and transfers. A bus or taxi may be easier for several large bags.

Is AREX better than the airport bus?

AREX is better for predictable rail time and hotels with a simple station connection. A bus can be better when an official stop is close to the hotel and reduces luggage transfers.

Can I buy an AREX ticket with a foreign credit card?

AREX lists support for major foreign-issued cards for Express tickets, and AREX station machines support credit-card purchase of All-Stop single-use cards. Carry a backup card or Korean won in case an issuer declines the transaction.

What happens if I miss the last train?

Check official night-bus and airport-bus services first. If none fits your destination and time, use an official airport taxi stand or a verified pickup.
